How the unit runs

The first two lessons settle mass against weight and are worth doing in order — almost every later mistake in this topic is really that distinction coming apart. The middle three widen the scale from the Earth–Moon system out to other galaxies. The last one supplies the unit you need to talk about any of it: the light year, which is a distance and not a time.

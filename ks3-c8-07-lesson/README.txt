Two things to know before reviewing:

  - Copper oxide reads pH 7 on the bench. That is correct and deliberate: it is
    a basic oxide that is insoluble, so nothing registers. It is the difference
    between a base and an alkali, and it is the point.
  - Water is on the tray as a non-metal oxide and reads pH 7. Also deliberate.

This is the law of conservation of mass: no atoms are lost or made during a chemical reaction, so the mass of the products equals the mass of the reactants. Bonds break and new bonds form, but the same atoms are only rearranged into new substances. That is why a symbol equation must be balanced: it shows the same number of atoms of each element on both sides.

Key fact

A big number in front multiplies the whole formula: 2H₂O is 4 H and 2 O. A small number inside belongs to the substance, and you never change it to balance.

!

Think again

“2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O has three molecules on the left and only two on the right. So it is not balanced, and the water must weigh less.”

A word equation names the substances. A symbol equation uses their formulae, and can add state symbols: (s) solid, (l) liquid, (g) gas and (aq) aqueous, meaning dissolved in water. The balancing numbers give the ratio of formula units that react, not the number of grams.

The same numbers are amounts in moles: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O means 2 moles of H₂ react with 1 mole of O₂ to give 2 moles of H₂O.

Equation

Learn it · no chemistry equation sheet

total mass of reactants = total mass of products

Rearranged, it finds any one missing mass: add up the side you know, then subtract the masses you have on the other side.

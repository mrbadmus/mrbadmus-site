Key words

Words to use in your answers

P-wave
A longitudinal seismic wave. Faster than S-waves. Travels through solids and liquids.
S-wave
A transverse seismic wave. Slower than P-waves. Travels through solids only.
Seismometer
An instrument that detects and records the vibrations from seismic waves.
Shadow zone
A region of the Earth's surface where a type of seismic wave is not detected.
Refraction
A change in direction caused by a change in wave speed, for example at a boundary between layers.
S–P interval
The time between the P-waves and the S-waves arriving. It is longer for seismometers further from the earthquake.

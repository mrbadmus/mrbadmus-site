## 2. What is recorded now

Family children only. A school pupil's writes are unchanged, and tests prove a school pupil never touches the new tables.

- **`child_answers`, an append-only ledger.** One row per answer on:
  - a lesson: Start-here guesses, every pick (including changed picks), choice, sort, chain, calculation and written rung;
  - practice, unit checks and exam questions.

  Each row holds:
  - a stable question id and the knowledge point (id and label);
  - the question **exactly as shown** and the child's answer;
  - the correct answer;
  - right/wrong, or marks scored out of marks available plus the points credited, plus the AQA level and next step on 6-markers;
  - the true attempt number, the measured time, and when.
- **Written answers and calculations are AI-marked at submit.** For calculations, the working is marked against the model's lines and the final answer against the key. An unmarked answer is stored "unmarked" and never counts. **The child's self-ticks are never the recorded mark** (proved: Ada ticked 4/5 "not yet", the record holds the AI's 6/6 Level 3; Ben ticked 2/4, the AI gave 1/4).
- **Lesson scores (`quiz_scores`) for family children** are rewritten on the server from marked ledger rows only (latest attempt per rung).
- **Measured time, one definition per surface:**
  - practice and exam: from when the question appeared;
  - lesson: from the child's previous recorded answer on that page;
  - hidden-tab time is excluded;
  - over 30 minutes is stored as unknown, never capped.
- **`child_tutor_questions`.** Each question, the reply exactly as shown, the lesson/page, and asked/replied times.
- **Parent endpoint `GET /api/consumer/children/:id/activity`.** Read-only, guardian-checked, all time by default. It returns:
  - every work item with every answer;
  - per-knowledge-point totals (answered / right / wrong / partial / unmarked, last seen, label);
  - tutor exchanges with replies;
  - `question_html` / `reply_html`, sanitised with the tutor's exact escape and allow-list.

  A child's own token gets 404; no token gets 401.
- **The tutor panel tells a family child:** "Your parent or tutor can see the questions you ask here." I wrote "or tutor" because organisation children use the same page; change it if you want "parent" only.
- **Resilience:** if the new tables fail or hang, every hook gives up after 3 s and the pupil's own request is byte-identical. Tested: 128 fault cases.

## 6. Sign-in rate limit

**Which limits apply:**
- **Child password sign-in** is `POST /auth/v1/token`: 150 per 5 minutes **per IP**, with a fixed burst of 30.
- **Every child sign-in comes from the backend's single IP.**
- **Token refresh** runs in each child's browser, from their home IP.
- **The "sign-ups and sign-ins" setting** (30 per 5 min) does **not** cover password sign-in.

**A realistic evening** (30 families, 2 children each, 2.5 attempts per child, 60% in 15 minutes) peaks at about **17%** of the limit. Even with 60% inside one 5-minute window it peaks at about **50%**.

**The real risk is deliberate.** Junk logins every 2 seconds could drain the shared allowance for everyone. The backend also trusted a spoofable leftmost `X-Forwarded-For`, so its own per-IP limits could be dodged.

**Fixed in code:**
- **Client IP:** now the rightmost *public* address.
- **Global safety net:** after 60 failed child sign-ins in 5 minutes across all addresses, unknown usernames get the normal 401 without calling Supabase.
- **Busy Supabase:** shows "Lots of people are signing in right now. Wait one minute, then try again. Your password is fine." It used to say the password was wrong.
- **Per-child-address limits:** with `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY` set, sign-in sends `sb-forwarded-for`, so Supabase limits each child's own address.

# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 19 September 2026**

This policy explains what personal information MrBadmus collects, why, and what we do with it. Because MrBadmus is used by children, we've tried to write it so that a parent and a child can both understand it. There's a short version for children at the end.

## 1. Who is responsible

The data controller is **3rd Eye Ltd**, trading as MrBadmus, company number 16404358, registered office 83 Windermere Road, Ince, Wigan, England, WN2 2NF. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number ZC252968.

Questions about privacy: **support@mrbadmus.com** (put "Privacy" in the subject line).

If you use MrBadmus through your school, your school is the data controller and its own privacy notice applies; this policy is for families and organisations who subscribe to MrBadmus directly.

## 2. What we collect

**About parents and organisation staff**
- Name and email address (and, for Google or Microsoft sign-in, the identifier your provider gives us).
- Login and security information (we store a secure hash of your password, never the password itself).
- Subscription and billing status. **Card details are held by Stripe, our payment processor; we never see or store your full card number.**
- Messages you send inside MrBadmus.
- Support emails you send us.
- Technical information: IP address, browser type, and logs of requests to our service, kept for security and to make things work.

**About children**
- First name, year group, exam board, tier and the mode chosen by the parent (alongside school or home education).
- The username the parent chose, and a secure hash of the password. **Children never need an email address.**
- Learning activity: lessons opened, questions answered, scores, time spent, streaks, unit-check results.
- Written answers to exam-style questions and the marks and feedback they receive.
- Questions asked to the AI tutor and the answers given.
- Messages between the child and their parent (or their organisation's staff).

We do not collect a child's surname, date of birth, address, photograph, location, or contact details. We don't use advertising trackers or sell data to anyone.

## 4. AI processing — what actually happens

Two features use an AI model provided by Anthropic:

- **Instant marking.** When a child submits an extended answer, we send the question, the mark scheme and the child's answer text to the model, which returns a mark and feedback. We do not send the child's name or username.
- **The tutor.** When a child asks the tutor a question, we send the question (and the lesson it relates to) to the model and show the answer.

Anthropic processes this content only to produce the response we ask for. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, content sent through their API is **not used to train their models**, and inputs and outputs are **automatically deleted within 30 days**. Anthropic may keep content longer — up to two years — only where a request is flagged for breaching their usage policy, or where the law requires it.

AI marking can be wrong. It's clearly labelled as instant marking, and answers a child sends to Mr Badmus are marked by a human teacher.

**Inside MrBadmus:** a parent sees everything about their own children and nothing about anyone else's. A child sees their own work and messages from their own parent (or organisation staff). Children cannot see each other. There are no public profiles and no cross-family leaderboards.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **While you subscribe:** everything, so the progress record is complete.
- **After your subscription ends:** the account is read-only for 30 days so you can retrieve reports, then personal data is deleted within 12 months of the end date. Anonymised, aggregated statistics may be kept.
- **If you ask us to delete sooner:** we do, within 30 days, apart from billing records we must keep for tax law (six years) and anything we're legally required to retain.
- **Messages:** kept as above; a message the sender deletes is hidden immediately and purged with the account.
- **Support emails:** two years.
- **Security logs:** 90 days.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask us to: show you the data we hold about you or your child; correct it; delete it; give you a copy in a portable form; or stop using it in a particular way. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw any consent you've given.

Most of this you can do yourself: the account page lets you download your family's data and delete the account. For anything else, email support@mrbadmus.com. We'll respond within one month and won't charge you.

**If you want to complain to us about how we've handled your or your child's personal data**, email support@mrbadmus.com with "Data complaint" in the subject line. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days of receiving it, tell you who is dealing with it, and respond substantively as quickly as we can.

If you're still unhappy after that, you can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. You don't have to come to us first, but we'd appreciate the chance to fix it.

## 8. Children's privacy

MrBadmus is designed for children, and we follow the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code:

- Accounts for children are created and controlled by a parent or guardian; children can't sign up on their own.
- We collect the minimum a child needs to learn: a first name, a year group, and their work.
- Privacy is high by default. Nothing a child does is public. Nothing is shared with other children.
- We don't use nudges, streak-shaming, or dark patterns to keep children on the platform. Streaks are frozen when a parent pauses work.
- Children never receive marketing.
- AI features are labelled, and human marking is available.
- A parent can see everything their child does and delete it at any time.

## 9. Cookies

MrBadmus uses only the cookies and browser storage it needs to keep you signed in and remember your settings. We don't use advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

## 10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access inside our database is controlled row by row so that each family can only ever reach its own data — this is enforced by the database itself, not just by the app. Passwords are hashed. Staff access to personal data is limited to what's needed to run the service and support you. If we ever suffer a breach that puts you or your child at risk, we'll tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

## 11. Changes

We'll email you before any change to this policy that affects you. The date at the top tells you when it was last updated.

## For children: the short version

- Your grown-up made your account. They chose your username and can see your work and messages. That's how it's meant to be.
- We keep your first name, your year group, and the work you do. We don't need your last name, birthday, address or photo, so we don't ask for them.
- Other children can't see your work, your name or your messages. Ever.

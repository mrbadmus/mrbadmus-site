# Question audit, 11 Nov 2026

What family children are set, against what they have been taught. Written for Mide and the platform chat. Read off production (read-only) on 11 Nov 2026.
## How selection worked before this fix

- **Practice** took the four standard questions of this week's lesson, then filled to ten from every earlier lesson in the year's scheme, whether or not the child had been set it. A plan that starts at the term week has many of those.
- **Exam questions** were chosen from the child's year and subject by topic, nearest their place in the scheme, with no check that the lesson had been taught.
- **Unit checks** tested every lesson in the unit.
- **The exam list** showed every KS3 question in the key stage.

## How it works now

Every family path reads `consumer/taught-scope.js`: this week's lessons first, then lessons the child has done (this week's subject first, nearest first), then lessons set but not done, inside the child's own year only. Nothing else. A practice that comes up short is honest and says how many questions it has. Questions on the exclusion list (below) are never set.

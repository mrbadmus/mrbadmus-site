# MRB-335 — Mide's five-minute live check (production, your login)

Do this on your phone at 390px first, then once on the laptop. One line per
step: what to do, then what you should see. Nothing here writes to any real
class except the last block, which uses 8r/Sc1 only.

## A. A separate-science class (any `…/Bi1`, `…/Ch1`, `…/Ph1` you teach or administer)

1. Teacher → My classes → open the class → **Set work**. Sheet opens on **Classes**; that class is ticked; only same-subject classes are listed below it.
2. **Next**. Step reads **Topic**; the header stays at the top while you scroll; **Next** is greyed until you pick something.
3. Tier chips read **Foundation | Higher** with **Higher** selected (for a `…1` set); no Subject chips, no Paper chips.
4. Only that science's topics are listed; a physics class shows no Inheritance, a biology class shows no Forces. Every row has a number on the right.
5. Tap **Foundation**. The numbers change; the list does not jump.
6. Scroll half-way, tap a topic row. It highlights; the list stays where it was.
7. Tap the chevron on that topic. Its subtopics open with their own numbers; tap one; the topic un-highlights and the subtopic highlights.
8. **Next**. Step reads **Detail**; chips **5 · 10 · 15 · 20** with **10** selected; ten questions listed, one line each.
9. Tap a question row. It expands to the full stem and four options **A–D** with the correct one marked; a formula shows a real subscript (CO₂, not CO2).
10. Tap **Swap** on that row. A different question replaces it; the rest do not move.
11. Title reads `Topic · Subtopic`; **Release** shows **Now** selected; **Due** shows a date seven days out and **18:00**.
12. **Back** twice, then **Cancel**. Nothing was set.

## B. A combined class (any `…/Sc…` KS4 class)

13. Open it → **Set work** → **Next**. Tier default is **Foundation** on a `Sc4`/`Sc5` set and **Higher** otherwise.
14. Subject chips **Biology · Chemistry · Physics · All** and Paper chips **Paper 1 · Paper 2 · Both** are present.
15. Tap **Physics** then **Paper 2**: Forces, Waves, Magnetism only; no Space physics anywhere on this class.
16. Tap **Chemistry**, **Paper 1**: Atomic structure, Bonding, Quantitative, Chemical changes, Energy changes.
17. Tap **All**, **Both**: every topic returns; the scroll position does not jump.
18. **Cancel**.

## C. A KS3 class (any Year 7–9)

19. Open it → **Set work** → **Next**. Tier chips read **Easy | Medium | Hard**, **Medium** selected; Subject chips present; no Paper chips.
20. Every unit shows a number ≥ 50 at every tier. **Cancel**.

## D. Set real work on 8r/Sc1 only

21. Open **8r/Sc1** → **Set work** → **Next** → tap a unit (whole unit) → **Next**.
22. Tap **15**. Fifteen questions; if a chip is greyed, the number on the topic row is smaller than that chip.
23. Change the title to `Live check`.
24. **Release → Later**: a date (tomorrow) and **07:00** appear; set the time to a few minutes from now, today.
25. **Due**: set the date to two days out and the time to **16:30**.
26. If the release date is before 14 Sep 2026, one line appears: `Assignments open 14 Sep 2026`, nothing else.
27. **Set work**. The sheet closes; one toast: `Live check · 8r/Sc1`; the class card updates.
28. Admin → Classes: every KS4 row shows a Tier and a Pathway selector; 8r/Sc1 (KS3) shows neither.

## E. As your student test identity, before and after release

29. Before the release time: the student class page shows no `Live check`; opening its link directly gives the not-found page.
30. After the release time (past the hold if it applied): `Live check` appears with fifteen questions and the due time you set, shown in UK time.
31. Answer two questions; the teacher's class page shows the attempt.

Delete or leave the 8r/Sc1 set as you prefer; it is your sandbox.

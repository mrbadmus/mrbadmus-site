## 4 · The constraint, verbatim

```sql
alter table public.assignment_questions
  add constraint one_pool_per_assignment
  check (band is not null and rung is null) not valid;
```

- **Test first** (`qeppkiswvclkkwbxmlok`): applied (after rehearsing the
  shipped band migration its schema was missing), then watched it **reject** a
  rung row (`23514 … violates check constraint "one_pool_per_assignment"`)
  and **accept** a band row.
- **Production** (`urklkrwevjtlfbwnipjn`): applied as migration
  `20260824214711`, `convalidated = false` confirmed from `pg_constraint`,
  then watched it **reject** a rung insert against the real demo assignment.
- `NOT VALID` is deliberate: the 18 Aug demo rows violate it and must not
  block it; it enforces on every new row. `VALIDATE` waits on the cleanup
  (§8). Attempts are deliberately unconstrained — `rung` on an attempt is a
  record of what a student answered.

## 6 · The frozen exception — awaiting Mide's ruling

**The class page's practice round serves recall+apply MCQs from
`ks3_ladder_questions`.** Its ruled owner pool (`ks3_cards`) holds
flashcards — front/back pairs with no options and no answer — and cannot
supply a scored round. A working surface is never broken mid-run to satisfy
a table, so it is frozen exactly as it reads: one serving read in
`shared/student-live.js`, plus `/api/class/practice` on the backend. Both
are named in the `pool_ownership` gate and bounded to exactly one serving
read per layer — a second one anywhere fails the build. Options when you
rule: bless it (the ladder mirror is the natural home of scored KS3 MCQs for
covered lessons), give the round its own pool, or retire the round.

The Weekly Challenge needed no freeze — it reads no KS3 pool.

## What a student can do this morning that they could not yesterday

**A KS3 class now gets work set, without anybody setting it.**

Yesterday there was exactly one assignment on the whole platform, and it had been put there
by hand, in a migration, in May. Nothing in the product could make another one. The page that
shows a student their assignment was built back in MRB-238 and had been sitting there for
three months pointing at nothing — a consumer with no producer.

As of tonight, when a student or a teacher opens a class and the current teaching week has no
assignment, the backend builds one out of your scheme of work, writes it down, and hands it
over. Everyone who asks after that gets the same one.

You can see the first one it made. On `8r/Sc1`, week 1 of 2026-27:

> **Breathing and gas exchange · Pressure · Chemical reactions**
> Due Thursday 3 September, 18:00. Four questions.

Four, not fifteen. That is the rule working, not the rule breaking, and it is worth
understanding because it is the thing most likely to look wrong to you:

- A KS3 week is three lessons, one per science. Week 1 is Biology *the gas exchange system*,
  Physics *pressure — force over area*, Chemistry *chemical vs physical change*.
- Only the Biology one has questions written for it. Physics has none at all; Chemistry has
  them for C1, C2 and now C3, but not for C4.
- One lesson supplies four questions at a given difficulty. Week one is allowed to be short —
  that is your own ruling — and there is nothing earlier in the year to top it up from.

So it shipped four honest questions rather than fifteen padded ones.

⚠️ **And from week 2, that class gets nothing at all** until Physics and C4-and-up are banked.
The producer refuses to ship a half-full assignment outside week one, which is also your
ruling. It says so clearly rather than failing. **This is the single biggest thing standing
between you and September**, and it is content, not code.

**A student's marks also stopped lying about them.** The route the assignment page submits to
was quietly throwing away six of the columns we added on Wednesday, and — worse — it was
recording "I don't know whether this is right" as "this is wrong". The two rungs a student
marks themselves were being counted against them. Fixed and proved: a submission with three
answers, one right, one wrong, one self-marked, now scores **1 out of 2**, not 1 out of 3.

**1. The Physics and C4+ question gap — this is the September blocker.**
924 questions exist across 77 lessons: all of Biology, and Chemistry C1, C2, C3. **There is
not one Physics question.** The scheme of work runs to week 35; the bank reaches about week 4
of it. Every week from 2 onward will produce nothing for `8r/Sc1` until that is filled. No

**2. Somebody has to build "hand it in".**
Covered above, and it is the reason the swap did not happen. The button is drawn, the route
exists and works, and nothing joins them. Until it does, the assignment page cannot go live
whatever else is true.

**3. The class leaderboard cannot be filled in honestly, and that one is a decision, not a
bug.**
The board Design drew is a four-week history — "3 WEEKS AT THE TOP", arrows for who moved up
since last week. The only cross-student figure a student is allowed to read is *this week,
eligible students only, as a percentage*: no per-week series exists, and RLS means a student
cannot compute one. Repeating this week's number across the other three, or zeroing them,
would put a made-up result next to a real child's name — which is the same fault your 21
August ruling took out of the split bar. So the board renders **empty** rather than
plausible-but-false. **This needs your ruling**: either it shows this week only, or something
has to start recording a weekly history.

## What this prompt got wrong

Four runs running, checking a premise has caught one. Tonight:

**"Only for classes in the current academic year" quietly assumed the year had started.**
It has not. The 2026-27 year opens on **1 September** — eleven days away. Every rule about
computing the current teaching week from the year's dates is silent about the days *before*
day one, and the working-year helper resolves to 2026-27 precisely because it looks 30 days
ahead. Ruled it the way the rest of the system already leans: before the year opens, the
current week is **week 1**. Otherwise the producer would have computed week zero and there
would have been nothing to demonstrate tonight.

**MRB-239's "four schema gaps" was three-quarters stale.** Three were already closed by
earlier runs. The one that mattered was not in that list at all: `assignment_questions.rung`
was `NOT NULL` with a check constraint, and a bank question has a *band*, not a rung — so
under your own 20 August ruling, a bank question could not physically be stored. That single
column would have stopped the build.

**"The assignment cannot ship — 980 feedback lines are missing" is not outstanding work.**
That was recorded as a blocker on Wednesday. Those 980 lines are the *right answer's*
explanation on every bank question and every ladder rung, and ruling 1a already settles it:
v1 ships with three. The distractor feedback — the part that actually teaches — is complete:
**2,520 wrong options, every one carrying its explanation. Not one gap.**

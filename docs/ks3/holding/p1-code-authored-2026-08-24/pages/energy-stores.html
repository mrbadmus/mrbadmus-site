Energy stores

A wind-up torch has no battery in it and it lights up anyway. Something in that torch was filled up when you turned the handle. The question is what.

Start here

Turn the handle for thirty seconds and it shines for two minutes.

A wind-up torch has no battery and nothing goes into it except the turning of your hand. Stop turning and it keeps shining for a while, then fades. Turn it again and it starts over.

While you were turning the handle, what was being filled?

A spring. Turning the handle winds it, and a wound spring is a filled store — the same kind of store as a stretched elastic band. The torch then lets it unwind slowly, and that is what runs the bulb. Nothing was made and nothing was destroyed; something was filled and then emptied.

Energy is not a substance. You cannot bottle it, and there is no jar of it anywhere. It is an amount, measured in joules, that goes with a situation — how fast something is going, how hot it is, how high it is, how far it is stretched, what it is made of. Change the situation and the amount changes with it. A store is one of those situations, and there are five worth knowing.

The accounts · five stores

Five situations that hold an amount

0 of 5 stores opened

Open each one. For every store, the two lines that matter are what fills it and what empties it — those are the two you will be asked for.

Pick a store to open it.

Kineticmovement

Fills when
Anything speeds up.
Empties when
Anything slows down.
Belongs to
the moving object

A cyclist freewheeling down a hill is filling a kinetic store the whole way down. At the bottom she brakes and it empties again.

Standing still is a kinetic store with nothing in it, not a missing store.

Thermaltemperature

Fills when
Anything gets hotter.
Empties when
Anything gets cooler.
Belongs to
the object that is hot

A mug of tea cooling on a desk is emptying a thermal store. The desk and the room are filling theirs by the same amount.

Cold is not a store. A cold thing has a thermal store with less in it than a hot one, and that is the whole difference.

Gravitationalposition in a field

Fills when
Anything is raised.
Empties when
Anything falls.
Belongs to
the object AND the Earth, together

Lifting a book onto a shelf fills a gravitational store. It stays filled while the book sits there, and empties the moment the book falls off.

This one does not live inside the book. It is about the book, the Earth, and the gap between them — take the Earth away and there is no store at all.

Elasticdistortion

Fills when
Anything is stretched, squashed or bent.
Empties when
It springs back.
Belongs to
the stretched or squashed object

The wind-up torch in the hook. Turning the handle winds a spring tighter, and letting it unwind runs the bulb.

The object has to spring back. Squashing a lump of clay fills no elastic store, because clay stays squashed.

Chemicalcomposition

Fills when
Substances are made whose atoms are arranged in a way that holds more.
Empties when
They react into substances that hold less.
Belongs to
the substances, as they are arranged now

Petrol, food and a battery are all chemical stores. Burning, digesting and using are all ways of emptying one.

It is about which substances exist, not about how much there is of them. A full tank and a half tank are the same substance with different amounts in it.

Five stores, ten lines. Every situation you meet for the rest of this unit fills at least one of them and empties at least one other.

Notice that not one of the five is called sound, light or electricity. That is deliberate, and it is what the next block is about.

The discrimination · this is the lesson

A store, or a way of getting there?

0 of 8 sorted

A store is something a situation HOLDS and you could come back tomorrow and find still there. A way of transferring is something that HAPPENS between two moments, and when it stops there is nothing left of it. Sort all eight, then read them back.

A storestill there tomorrowA way of transferringonly while it is happening
  • A skateboard rolling downhill

    A kinetic store, filling as it speeds up. Stop the clock at any moment and there is an amount there.

  • Sound

    Sound is air being pushed back and forth by something vibrating. Stop the vibration and there is no sound left anywhere — nothing was stored, something was carried.

  • An oven at 200 degrees

    A thermal store, and a full one. Switch it off and come back in ten minutes; it is still hot, so it is still there.

  • Light

    Light is how a lamp reaches the wall. The wall's thermal store fills while the lamp is on and stops filling the instant it goes off. Nothing anywhere holds light.

  • A charged phone battery

    A chemical store. Leave the phone in a drawer for a week and the charge is still there, because the substances inside it are still the ones that hold more.

  • An electric current

    A current is charge moving round a circuit. Open the switch and the current is gone at once — what is left is the battery's chemical store, which is the thing that was holding anything.

  • A stretched elastic band

    An elastic store. Hold it stretched all afternoon and it is still loaded, which is exactly what a store means.

  • Heating a pan on a hob

    Heating is the hob's thermal store emptying into the pan's. It is the route between two stores, and when the hob is off there is no heating left over.

Sort every row, and the reasons open underneath.

Now the eight are read back. How many did you have in the right bin?

The four ways of transferring are sound, light, an electric current and heating. If those were the ones that caught you, that is the exact thing this block is for — and it catches most people the first time.

Four stores, four ways of getting from one store to another. The stores are what hold; the ways are what carry.

The word for the second column is a pathway, and there are only four of them in the whole of physics: heating, an electric current, a force moving something, and radiation — which is what light is.

Key fact

There are five stores worth knowing: kinetic, thermal, gravitational, elastic and chemical. Sound, light and electric current are not stores — they are how energy gets from one store to another.

Think again

“Sound, light and electricity are kinds of energy that things store.”

A speaker is playing loudly in an empty hall. Commit before you read on.

Nothing in the hall stores sound. The cone of the speaker pushes the air, the air pushes the next bit of air, and that travelling push is what your ear picks up. Cut the power and the hall is silent within a fraction of a second, because there was never anything holding it.

What the walls do absorb is real, and it is worth being exact about: the pushing warms them very slightly, so the walls' thermal store fills. Sound went in; a thermal store came out. That is a pathway ending at a store, which is what every pathway does.

Key note

Energy is an amount that goes with a situation, measured in joules. Five situations hold it: movement, temperature, height, stretch and what a substance is made of. Sound, light and electric current are not stores; they are how one store empties into another.

Going further

"A change in position in a field" is one phrase covering more than gravity. Push two north poles of two magnets together and let go: they fly apart, so something was filled while you pushed. Rub a balloon on your jumper and hold it near the wall and the same thing is true of electric charge. Both are the same idea as lifting a book — you moved something to where a field did not want it, and the store filled by exactly what it cost you. You will meet the magnetic one properly in Magnetism and electromagnetism and the charge one in Static electricity.

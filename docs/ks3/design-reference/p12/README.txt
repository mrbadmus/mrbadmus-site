Three lessons run CFIFA — p12-01, p12-02 and p12-06. Each carries a formula
triangle, two worked examples (one where nothing needs converting, one where a
mass in grams, a time in minutes or a pressure in kilopascals must be converted
first) and two write-it-out student attempts labelled only Question 1 and
Question 2, so deciding whether to convert is left to the student.

p12-02 is declared CONTRAST rather than QUANTITATIVE and still carries the
block: W = m × g is the whole content of the contrast, and the gram-to-kilogram
trap is where the distinction between mass and weight is actually lost.

p12-03, p12-04 and p12-05 have no calculation in them and no worked example has
been invented to fill the slot.

Every lesson carries four rail stops, a four-rung mastery ladder, two
misconception quotes, one [data-key-fact] block and a key note.

Delivery notes are in NOTES-P11-P12.md.

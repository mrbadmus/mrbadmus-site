CODE'S JOB — NOT BUILT HERE, DESIGNED FOR
  RECALL BANK. 8 questions ship in this file as the design sample. The
  live bank should be HUNDREDS, generated from every lesson the class
  has covered; six are drawn per round. The surface makes no assumption
  about bank size — it asks for round(n) and renders six. Suggested
  selection policy: weight toward questions this student got wrong (the
  same signal the flashcards' "from your work" marker uses), then
  least-seen, then random, and never repeat inside a round.
  FLASHCARD DECK. 6 cards ship as the sample; the deck takes any length.
  One fixed cost: the progress pips divide the full width, so past
  roughly 24 cards drop the pip row and keep the NN / NN counter — no
  other change needed.
  COUNTERS. ANSWERED / BEST STREAK / ROUNDS are zero-padded to two
  digits; widen to three if a keen student passes 99 in a week.
  THEME PERSISTENCE. Chosen once, remembered: write the student pref to
  data-bench-theme on the page root.
  WRONG-ANSWER TARGETING. The "FROM YOUR WORK" chip is already placed on

  REWARD SURFACE. bench-reward-slot is a real, empty 64px region in the

DESIGN DECISIONS WORTH KNOWING
  The docket never takes the theme. Paper + ink on all six grounds keeps
  the marking moment the strongest thing on the page, and stops the
  theme choice from changing how a mark reads.
  The light theme (Chalk) exists so a student who finds any dark panel
  heavy is not forced into one; it keeps the bench's presence through
  the striped edge and the ink title rather than through darkness.
  Graphite is kept, unrenamed in spirit — it is the old default, now a
  choice.
  Colour is never the only signal: every recall state carries a word and
  a drawn mark, every week state a label, every card a tag.

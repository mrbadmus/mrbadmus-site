NOTES-C6.md is the FULL C6 notes file, not an extract. What it says about this
lesson, and where:

  - Section 1: this lesson carries NO statutory statement of its own. Read
    section 2 before reviewing it.
  - Section 2: architecture §7 puts "Acid + alkali: making a salt" in this
    slot. This build has acids-and-carbonates here instead, and folds the
    acid + alkali salt work into c6-03 and c6-06. The case for the change is
    that acid + carbonate is the third of the three acid reaction families, it
    carries the limewater test, and without it the unit teaches two reactions
    of acids and calls it a set. It also sets up C10's carbonate chemistry.
  - Instruments: a five-step rig reveal (step-rig) plus a four-solid sorter
    (solid-sorter). Both DOM. Six rail stops, not the usual five.
  - Misconception ACID-08 — "a gas that puts out a splint is carbon dioxide" —
    is confronted here. It is NOS-shaped: it is about what counts as a specific
    test, not about carbonates. Flagged in the notes as the sixth
    nature-of-science misconception parked in a content family.

Science flag 10 in the notes applies to this lesson: limewater going CLEAR
again with excess carbon dioxide, at step 5 of the rig. That is correct
apparatus behaviour and is deliberately included. Most textbooks omit it —
confirm you want it at KS3.

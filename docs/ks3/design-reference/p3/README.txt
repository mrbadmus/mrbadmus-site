Standing conventions, same as B1:
  - Progress rail ticks only on completed activities, right or wrong.
  - One KEY FACT box per lesson. Amber is reserved for misconceptions.

This unit is the first physics anywhere in the course and the first
quantitative teaching in it. Every formula follows the four-part ruling:
formula alone in its own block, drawn as a triangle, worked example
revealed one step at a time, student fills in the steps before any full
FIFA question.

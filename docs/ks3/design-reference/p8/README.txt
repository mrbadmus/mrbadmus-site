KS3 P8 — Electric circuits
========================================

Each lesson is one standalone HTML file. Open any of them directly in a
browser; nothing needs to be installed and nothing is fetched from the
network. The design-system files each page needs sit in the _ds folder
beside them, and support.js is the runtime.

Lessons in slot order:

p8-01-current-and-circuits.dc.html                   Current and circuits
p8-02-series-and-parallel.dc.html                    Series and parallel
p8-03-current-at-a-junction.dc.html                  Current at a junction
p8-04-potential-difference.dc.html                   Potential difference
p8-05-resistance.dc.html                             Resistance
p8-06-conductors-and-insulators.dc.html              Conductors and insulators
p8-07-building-and-measuring-a-circuit.dc.html       Building and measuring a circuit

The four formula lessons — p8-03, p8-04, p8-05 and p8-06 — run CFIFA:
Convert, Formula, Insert, Fine-tune, Answer, revealed one step at a time.
Each carries two worked examples (one where nothing needs converting, one
where a milliamp or millivolt reading must be divided by 1000 first) and
two student attempts labelled only Question 1 and Question 2, so deciding
whether to convert is left to the student. Seven of those eight attempts
are write-it-out — five empty boxes, one per step, checked against the
model lines and self-marked. p8-06's second question keeps the
pick-the-line variant.

NOTES-P8-P9.md carries the delivery record for both units: statutory
ownership, the formula-block rulings, every bench's state space, the
pre-allocated misconception ids, the review flags and the component
families registered for the coverage gate.

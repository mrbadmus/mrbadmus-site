Keep the folder structure intact: each lesson loads support.js and the

The unit runs as a separation toolkit: 01 sets the pure/mixture question,
02 explains why anything dissolves at all, 03-06 are the four techniques in
order of what they can separate, and 07 turns the toolkit back on itself —
melting point as proof, not appearance.

Standing conventions, same as B1, P3, B2, C1 and C2:
  - Progress rail ticks only on completed activities, right or wrong.
  - One KEY FACT box per lesson. Amber is reserved for misconceptions.
  - Only the mastery ladder marks correctness.
  - No year or half-term appears anywhere in a lesson page.

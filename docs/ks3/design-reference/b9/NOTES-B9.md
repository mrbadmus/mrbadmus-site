## 1. New instruments

### 1.1 `chain-ledger` — flagship of `b9-01`
Three chains of different lengths (field 4, wood 4, sea 5); step up a level at a
time; energy falls by a factor of ten each step from 10,000 kJ. The verdict line
computes the final figure and percentage from the chain length, so adding a
chain needs no new prose.

### 1.2 `cycle-runner` — flagship of `b9-02`
A damped two-species model (r 0.6, K 2000, a 0.0015, b 0.35, m 0.35) starting at
800 prey and 120 predators, drawn as a 26-year twin-bar chart with the two series
scaled independently. **The carrying capacity K is load-bearing**, not decoration:
it is what makes the *remove every fox* button teach `ECO-06` rather than show
an exponential curve. Buttons: one year, ten years, remove/restore predators.

### 1.3 `remove-a-species` — flagship of `b9-03`
An eight-species oak-wood web; remove one of six and reveal three rounds of
consequences, then a verdict counting how far it reached. Bees are included
deliberately as a species with **no feeding line into the web at all** — their
removal still empties it, which is the cleanest possible setup for b9-04.

### 1.4 `supermarket-shelf` — flagship of `b9-04`
Twelve foods with a pollinator-dependence value and a share of the shelf's
calories and of its vitamins. Removing pollinators drops the calorie bar a
little and the vitamin bar a lot. **The gap between the two bars is the entire
lesson** — do not let a revision merge them into one "food" bar.

### 1.5 `bioaccumulation` — flagship of `b9-05`
Five trophic levels from 0.003 ppm in lake water; the multiplier is set by a
persistence dial (×10 persistent, ×3 partly broken down, ×1 excreted). The ×1
setting produces a flat line and is the control that proves the mechanism is
persistence rather than toxicity.

### 1.6 `quadrat-bench` — flagship of `b9-06`
A 10×10 field generated once on load with the daisies clustered towards one
corner. Placement (random / flowery corner / path edge) × sample size (3 / 8 /
25), then *show the real total*. **The two failure modes are separated on
purpose**: changing the sample size fixes the random-placement case and does
nothing at all for the two biased ones, which is the point of `ECO-12`.

## 2. Science flags — numbered for review

1. **The 10% rule** (b9-01 throughout). Stated as "about a tenth", with a legal
   line saying real efficiencies run from a few per cent to around twenty.
   Confirm — and note this is the same figure B7 flag 19 raised, now owned here.
2. **The three chains** and their organisms (b9-01). Plausible British examples.
   Confirm the sea chain runs to orca rather than stopping at seal.
3. **"A pair of blue tits needs about a hundred caterpillars a day"** (b9-01,
   b9-03). Usually quoted higher for a full brood. Confirm or replace.
4. **Whales eating krill two levels up** (b9-01 *Going further*). Confirm.
5. **The predator–prey model's parameters** (b9-02). Invented for teaching, and
   the legal line says so. Confirm that a damped model that converges is
   preferable to a neutral one that cycles for ever — I chose damped because it
   does not look like perpetual motion.
6. **The Hudson's Bay lynx–hare data** (b9-02 *Going further*). **RESOLVED,
   16 Aug.** Ruling: teach the linked cycle, because that is what the data show;
   do not teach that lynx control hare numbers, since hares cycle for reasons
   well beyond lynx; and Going further keeps the point that the record is
   trapping returns rather than a population count. The paragraph has been
   rewritten to that shape — the ledger finding is stated positively, the
   single-cause reading is refused, and the catch-not-census point stays as
   evidence handling rather than as a hedge on the model.
7. **The oak wood web** (b9-03): eight species, six removals, three rounds each.
   Every outcome is a plausible direction, not a prediction, and the legal line
   says so. Confirm the *remove the oak* case is worth including — it is the
   only one where the web does not reorganise.
8. **Yellowstone wolves** (b9-03 *Going further*). **RESOLVED, 16 Aug.** Ruling:
   teach that removing a top predator reaches far past its prey, on the
   elk-and-willow link the evidence actually supports; drop *the wolves changed
   the rivers*. With the overclaim gone the caveat had nothing left to correct,
   so it has gone too. The paragraph now runs wolf → elk numbers and elk
   behaviour → willow and aspen → beaver → the water, and closes on chain versus
   web. Note that this removes the unit's only outbound link to `b6-03`.
9. **The Einstein "four years" quotation** (b9-04 hook) — misattributed, and the
   lesson says so. Confirm you are happy to open a lesson by naming a false
   quotation.
10. **The twelve foods and their dependence values** (b9-04). Directions are
    right; the numbers are illustrative. In particular tomatoes are given as 0.7
    (buzz-pollinated, and glasshouse growers buy bumblebees), and milk as 0.15
    via insect-pollinated fodder crops. Confirm both, since both are arguable.
11. **"More managed honeybee colonies now than fifty years ago"** and the claim
    that wild pollinators are the ones declining (b9-04 think-again). Well
    supported globally; confirm the framing.
12. **Hand pollination in Sichuan** (b9-04 *Going further*). Real, and the cause
    is genuinely disputed, which the lesson says. Confirm.
13. **The bioaccumulation figures** (b9-05): 0.003 ppm to 25 ppm across five
    levels, harm threshold 1 ppm. Same order as published DDT figures, not from
    a study. Confirm the numbers and the threshold.
14. **DDT and eggshell thinning, Silent Spring, 1962, and continued indoor use
    for malaria control** (b9-05 *Going further*). All correct. **Confirm you
    want the last clause** — it is the sentence that stops the story being a
    morality tale, and it is also the one that could be quoted out of context.
15. **The tuna and swordfish mercury advice** (b9-05 rung 4). Real public health
    advice. Confirm it is appropriate as a rung rather than as a flagged health
    claim.
16. **Capture–mark–recapture** (b9-06 *Going further*), including trap-shy and
    trap-happy animals. Confirm the depth at KS3 — it is not in the programme of
    study but it is what a student will ask about immediately.
17. **No diagrams anywhere in B9.** A drawn food web is the obvious candidate
    and is **not in the diagram manifest**. b9-03 currently gives the web as a
    list of who-eats-whom lines, which works and is not what a textbook would
    do. **Flag: a proper web diagram would improve b9-01 and b9-03 more than
    any other illustration in the biology build.**

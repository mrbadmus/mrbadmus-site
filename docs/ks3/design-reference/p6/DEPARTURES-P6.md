## Not departures, and why

Everything below is this lane's own defect or the engine's. None is a change
to what Design drew, and none takes a row.

**Two dead rail stops.** `p6-08`'s `#s-chart` and `p6-09`'s `#s-uses` were
authored with `sibling` / `sibling_at`; the drawer reads `band_anchor` /
`band_at` and ignored both in silence. ⚠️ **MRB-208's gate cannot see this
failure**: a band section ships `data-stage-done="0"`, which IS a signal
`doneByDom()` reads, so the stop looks reachable and never becomes true.
`_sibling` now raises on a near-miss key.

**A third dead stop, and a mechanism that already existed.** `p6-03`'s
`#s-stages` ticks on the HOOK — Design's own DONE for it reads
`s.hookChoice !== null` — and there is no instrument above it to give it a
sibling tick. A bespoke `after_anchor` was built for it, watching the hook
section's DOM and writing this section's own `data-stage-done`. It worked,
and it was still wrong twice over.

⚠️ **MRB-249's gate said so, and it was right.** That gate reads Design's
`isDone()` as a MIRROR MAP and compares it with what the rail DECLARES, so a
stop ticking by a private route fails it: *"how-sound-is-made.html: mirror
map is {}, Design's isDone() gives {'s-stages': 's-hook'}"*. `ks3_parity`'s
own `_TICK_EXEMPT` note had already written the answer down — *"whoever takes
it: the answer is probably `mirrors`, not a new predicate"* — and this lane
wrote the new predicate anyway, having read neither. The stop now carries
`"mirrors": "s-hook"` in its rail row; `wireRail` resolves it, MRB-208's R2
accepts it through the hook's own reachability, and `after_anchor`,
`data-after` and `wireBandAfter` are all gone.

**The log-range bench had no notes at all** — three branch templates
authored, neither validated nor emitted. The drawer now refuses a payload
missing a branch, or one whose branch names no second listener.

**The gauge's depth slider did nothing** — emitted as
`data-fgauge-slider="d"`, queried as `"depth"`. The liveness gate could not
see it: pressing a material tab moves the block, and the block is what that
gate watches.

**The slinky called a compression a rarefaction on 17 of 21 positions.** A
longitudinal wave's crowding is in the GRADIENT of the displacement, not the
displacement — the coils are packed where a coil's own displacement is zero.
The label contradicted the drawing beside it. Design's `lessonVals()` keys on
`cosv` with ±0.5, and ±0.85 on the sine for the transverse case; **both
thresholds and her wording are now hers**.

**Two internal lesson slugs in student prose**, caught by `ks3_smoke
--static`. **Five authored keys read by nothing**, two of them Design's own
captions that had never been drawn. **One incomplete `rest` map**, refused by
`kit._rest_fill`. **Two cover-bar payloads in the wrong shape**, refused by
the build. **One time formatter** that switched to milliseconds below a
second where hers stays in seconds — which reads as a different quantity from
the one the triangle just produced.

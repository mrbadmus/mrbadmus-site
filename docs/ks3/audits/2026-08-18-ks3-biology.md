# KS3 Biology — live-site audit

**Scope:** all 58 live lessons across B1–B11, plus the subject hub and 11 unit indexes (70 pages).
Audited against **mrbadmus.com**, not the source records. Read-only: nothing was written to the
repo, which stayed at `1ccfef858` with a clean tree throughout.

**Method:** 17 agents across 6 workstreams. Every instrument driven in headless Chrome through
every reachable state, twice where a second run could differ; layout measured at 390 / 768 /
1440 px (plus 320 px reflow and 640×512 for 200% zoom) via device-metrics override; contrast
measured against the resolved painted ground including `opacity` compositing, not from CSS
source. Headline findings were independently re-verified before inclusion.

**Baseline that holds everywhere:** all 58 lessons render with **zero console errors**, zero
uncaught exceptions, and **CLS 0.0000**. With JavaScript disabled a lesson still serves
6,261–7,603 characters of teaching prose and every MCQ option — the pages are genuinely
server-rendered. Brand markup is byte-identical across all 82 fetched files with zero drift.

## CROSS-CUTTING ROOT CAUSES

These account for a large share of the individual findings below. Each is one fix.

**C1 · `shared/ks3.js` — five head counters are never updated.** `wireGasCompare`, `wireBellJar`,
`wireCrossingCounter`, `wireFaultBench` and `wireTwoProcessLedger` call `markStage()` but never
`setCount()`. Every other unit's wire function does. The authored `data-on` strings ("light
adjusted", "model worked", "switches used") are unreachable dead bytes. Verified live: the
stomata header reads **"currently dark"** while the light readout beside it reads **100 units**.
*Fix: add the missing `setCount(sec, …)` at the end of each `draw()`/`onMove()`.*

**C2 · `shared/ks3.js:375` — the mastery ladder marks a lesson finished after ONE rung.**
`if (resolved) { markVisit(slug, true); }` where `resolved` is a **count**, and the comment two
lines above reads *"all four rungs resolved means finished"*. On all 58 lessons.
*Fix: `if (resolved === rungs.length)`.*

**C3 · The ladder score line claims work not done this sitting.** On load with a stored best,
the ladder reads *"You got 2 of 4. Your best so far is 4 of 4."* before the student touches
anything; the resting note is also overwritten with the past-tense *"You marked rungs 3 and 4
yourself."* while the score still says "Not started yet." All 58 lessons.
*Fix: suppress the score/note lines until `resolved > 0` this sitting.*

**C4 · Build metadata is printed to students on 11 lessons.** Pattern: *"… is declared in the
lesson record as `b5-pollen-tube`, awaiting illustration."* All 8 reproduction lessons plus
`the-digestive-system`, `absorption-and-the-small-intestine`, `the-gas-exchange-system`.
Violates the project's own §8.10 rule. *Fix: emit nothing when the asset is unbuilt.*

**C5 · The figure pipeline is declared but not wired.** 15 figure records sit at
`status: "needed"` across 12 lesson files. `SVG_ART` registers only three drawers (`food-web`,
`base-pairs`, `moth-pair`) and only 4 `{"type":"figure"}` blocks are placed in `core[]`. The 15
render **nothing at all** — not even the `ks3-figure-pending` placeholder that exists in the
codebase. The only trace a student sees is the C4 footnote.
*Fix: landing any diagram needs both an `SVG_ART` drawer AND a figure block placed in `core[]`.*

**C6 · The bench-intro paragraph narrates the controls.** In 11 of 19 benches across B7–B11,
`#s-bench > p` opens by describing buttons already on screen. `disturbing-a-food-web` ships **no**
intro paragraph and loses nothing — the evidence that these can be cut outright, not trimmed.

**C7 · `Four rungs. Two the page marks, two you mark.`** on all 58 lessons, and duplicated in the
same header block by `.ks3-score-note` ("Rungs 3 and 4 you mark yourself"), which says it better.
*Fix: drop one, once, in the generator.*

# WS2 · INCONSISTENCIES

**First, the good news:** the original b9-05 defect (prose saying 25 ppm over a bench reaching
300) is **fixed**. No "25 ppm" survives anywhere in the corpus; `toxic-build-up-in-a-food-chain`
now reconciles fully. But the *class* recurred — see 5.5, 5.8, 5.11, 5.16, 5.18, 2.4 below.

## ships-broken

**2.1 · Mitochondria — B8 contradicts B1.**
> **B8** `aerobic-respiration`: *"It happens in the mitochondria of **every living cell**, continuously"* — and again in the summary. Plus: *"**Every living cell has them**… Not in the lungs, not in the blood — in the cells themselves."*
> **B1** `specialised-cells`, red blood cell: *"**It has none either** — which means it cannot use any of the oxygen it is carrying. A courier that ate the parcel would be no use."*

**Ruling: B1 is correct.** Mature mammalian RBCs expel every organelle — which is exactly the
point B1 builds a whole panel on. B1 also establishes bacteria as living cells with no
membrane-bound organelles, and B8's own next lesson has those bacteria respiring. B8's universal
quantifier is the error. *Fix: "almost every cell — the red blood cell is the exception you met in
Specialised cells, and it is why it cannot use its own cargo."*

**2.2 · Gas exchange "only in the alveoli" — B4 contradicts B5.**
> **B4** `the-gas-exchange-system`: *"it is the **only place in the body** where that happens"* (and in the KEY FACT: *"it happens **only in the alveoli**"*)
> **B5** `gestation-placenta-and-birth`: *"The lungs are full of fluid for the whole pregnancy and **no gas exchange takes place in them**"*

**Ruling: B5 is correct; B4's claim is unscoped.** For a foetus the alveoli do none of it and the
placenta does all of it — and B4's own unit contains a lesson titled *Stomata and gas exchange in
plants*. *Fix: "the only place in your lungs… once you are breathing air."*

**2.3 · `a-balanced-diet` — the Key fact contradicts its own bench.**
> **Key fact:** *"**Three** of the seven are measured in hundreds of grams. **Three** are measured in milligrams."*
> **The bench's own scoring copy:** *"two nutrients in hundreds of grams, **three in tens**, two in milligrams."*

Answer key measured live: `lots, some, some, trace, trace, some, lots` = **2 / 3 / 2**. The Key
fact is wrong on both halves. *Fix: match the bench.*

**2.4 · `enzymes-in-digestion` — the rule card and the bench disagree about protease at pH 8.**
> **Rule card:** *"Best at pH 2 in the stomach, **8 in the small intestine**"*
> **Bench, measured:** protease @ pH 8 → **`Rate 0% of maximum`**, 0 units made, verdict *"the conditions are simply wrong for it"*

Cause: `opt_ph: {protease: 2}` — one scalar optimum — with `ph_span: 4.5`, so |8−2| = 6 → 0. Rung
3 reinforces the card (*"raising the pH to around 8"*). **Ruling: the card is right** (pepsin ~2,
trypsin ~8; it is why the lesson teaches the pancreatic alkali at all, and it is what AQA asks).
The model needs a per-enzyme optimum **set**. The one pairing the lesson most wants a student to
try is the one the bench denies.

**2.5 · `unicellular-organisms` — the hook says a cheek cell and a Paramecium are the same size;
the page says one is four times the other.**
> **Hook:** *"A cheek cell is about the same size… **Neither is bigger than the other**"* — under an h2 reading *"A quarter of a millimetre long, and it hunts."*
> **Instrument:** *"Your cheek cell is **0.06 mm** and a Paramecium is **0.25 mm**"*
> **Ladder rung 1:** *"A Paramecium is **four times the size** of your cheek cell"*

Engine confirms `CHEEK_D = 0.06`. **Ruling: 0.06 vs 0.25 mm is correct** (buccal squamous ~50–60 µm;
*P. caudatum* 200–300 µm). The hook is wrong, and its whole rhetorical move rests on it.

**2.6 · `a-balanced-diet` — water called one of the four "needed in far smaller amounts".**
Key note: *"The other four are needed in **far smaller amounts**"* (= vitamins, minerals, fibre,
**water**). Bench row 6: *"about 2000 g — Two kilograms — **by far the largest requirement on the
list**"*. Water is ~6× the carbohydrate figure on the same bench.

## Left alone deliberately — the science would move

Recorded so these are not "fixed" later by someone reading only the list above.

- **Every figure hedge**, on every page: `about 7 metres`, `roughly 70 m²`, `About 500 million
  alveoli`, `about one unit an hour`, `roughly a day`, `about a fortnight`, `close to a fortnight
  in almost everyone`, `about 16% oxygen`, `roughly 200 times more strongly`, `about a tenth of
  the energy at each level`, `around thirty trillion`, `about 160 newtons`, `something like nine
  tenths of marine species`, `tens to hundreds of times the background rate`. Cutting any of these
  makes a true statement false.
- **Condition hedges:** `Composition figures are rounded typical values for a person **at rest**`;
  `Weight in newtons is taken as mass × 10 N/kg throughout`; `mostly on the underside`.
- **`almost without exception, unable to have offspring`** (what-makes-a-species) — the footnote
  says "almost always infertile" because fertile female mules are recorded. Tightening makes it false.
- **The whole `Individuals do not change — populations do` cluster** in `natural-selection`
  (*"Nothing is choosing"*, *"no need, no goal, no trying"*, *"it cannot look ahead"*). Reads as
  repetition; is the precision the entire unit turns on.
- **All safeguarding wording**, including the apparently redundant: `and a pharmacist will answer
  a question about a substance without an appointment and **without a lecture**` — "without a
  lecture" is doing real work for a fourteen-year-old deciding whether to ask. Also
  `It is a teaching model and must not be used to judge whether anyone is fit to drive.`,
  `Risk is a statement about populations. It is not a verdict on a person.`, `vapes are very
  likely less harmful than cigarettes **and are not known to be safe**`, and the RSE/PSHE routing
  footers repeated across all five human-reproduction lessons — a student may meet any one page in
  isolation, so the repetition **is** the safeguard.
- **Model-limitation disclosures:** `The bench is a simplified model: rate is shown against
  temperature and pH only`; `The bench is a simulation of the practical, not a substitute for it,
  and it is not a risk assessment.`; `plausible directions rather than predictions`; `illustrative
  outcomes … not projections`. These are the pages being honest about their own models.
- **Functional instruction that looks like narration:** `Tap a row to see why.` — the rows are
  `button.ks3-cmp-btn` styled as table rows with the explanation `hidden`; without the sentence
  the affordance is invisible. Same for `Make your prediction first — then the lab runs.`
- **`Commit before you check` / `Commit to an answer before you check it`** — predict-before-reveal
  is the pedagogy, not scaffolding round it. If it goes, it should be stated once in the UI, not
  edited out of individual lessons.
- **MCQ options and distractors** — e.g. `Oxygen moves in order to spread out evenly`. Editing an
  option changes the assessment, not the prose; making a distractor crisper makes it more persuasive.

**S1 · `chromosomes-genes-and-dna` — "a factor of a thousand at every step" is false at every

|---|---|---|
| person → cell | 1.6 m → 0.02 mm | **×80,000** |
| cell → nucleus | 0.02 → 0.006 mm | **×3.3** |
| nucleus → chromosome | 0.006 → 0.002 mm | **×3** |
| gene → bases | 0.002 → 0.0000003 mm | **×6,667** |

*Ruling: the ladder is not evenly spaced and no step is ~1,000. Either cut the sentence or replace
with a claim the column supports.* Found independently by three agents.

**S2 · Mitochondria in "every living cell"** — see **2.1**. *Ruling: B1 is right; mature mammalian

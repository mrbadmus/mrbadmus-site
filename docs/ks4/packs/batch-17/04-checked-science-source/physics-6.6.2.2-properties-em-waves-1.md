## higher

Explain refraction in terms of the change in wave speed at a boundary — when a wave slows it bends towards the normal. Describe total internal reflection (TIR): when light in a denser medium hits a boundary at an angle greater than the critical angle, all light reflects internally. Critical angle: sin(c) = 1/n. Applications: optical fibres, endoscopes, diamond cut.

## common_mistake

When light enters glass from air, it SLOWS DOWN and bends TOWARDS the normal (angle of refraction < angle of incidence). When it leaves glass into air, it SPEEDS UP and bends AWAY from the normal. Always identify which medium is faster/slower.

## key_note

Refraction: change in direction due to change in speed at boundary. Air → glass (slower): bends towards normal. Glass → air (faster): bends away. Frequency constant; wavelength changes. Ray diagrams: incident ray, normal, refracted ray, angles of incidence and refraction.

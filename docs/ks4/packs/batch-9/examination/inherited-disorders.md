## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**
- Swapping which disorder is dominant and which recessive.
- "Carriers have mild CF."
- "CF is caught" / an infection.
- "A dominant disorder must be common."
- Ethics answers with one side only, or no economic point (cost of screening vs cost of lifelong care) when "economic" is asked.
- "Embryo screening cures the disorder" — it identifies embryos; gene therapy is the attempt to treat.

**Command words**: Give, Describe, Explain why … can skip a generation, Evaluate / Discuss (embryo screening — economic, social, ethical, using given information), Complete the Punnett square, Determine the probability.

**Required practical**: none.

**Calculations**: probability from a single-gene cross (CF Ff × Ff → 1/4; polydactyly Dd × dd → 1/2). No units; Convert = "nothing to convert". Completing = base; constructing = HT.

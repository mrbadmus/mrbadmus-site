Spec statement (verbatim, 8464 = 8462) 5.4.3.5: "During electrolysis, at the cathode (negative electrode), positively charged ions gain electrons and so the reactions are reductions. At the anode (positive electrode), negatively charged ions lose electrons and so the reactions are oxidations. Reactions at electrodes can be represented by half equations, for example: 2H+ + 2e- → H2 and 4OH- → O2 + 2H2O + 4e- or 4OH- – 4e- → O2 + 2H2O"
5.4.3.1: "(HT only) Throughout Section 4.4.3 Higher Tier students should be able to write half equations for the reactions occurring at the electrodes during electrolysis, and may be required to complete and balance supplied half equations."

## summary

## higher

Qualitative Le Chatelier's principle: concentration increase shifts equilibrium to use up added species. Temperature increase shifts in endothermic direction. Pressure increase shifts to side with fewer moles of gas. Catalyst speeds up reaching equilibrium but doesn't change the position.

## common_mistake

At equilibrium, the reaction has NOT stopped — it is DYNAMIC. Both forward and reverse reactions continue at equal rates. Concentrations are CONSTANT but not necessarily EQUAL. Students often say 'equilibrium means equal concentrations' — this is wrong. It means equal RATES.

## key_note

Reversible reaction: ⇌ symbol. Products can reform reactants. Closed system → dynamic equilibrium: forward and reverse rates equal, concentrations constant (not equal). If forward is exothermic → reverse is endothermic (same energy magnitude). NH₄Cl decomposition and CuSO₄ hydration are key examples.

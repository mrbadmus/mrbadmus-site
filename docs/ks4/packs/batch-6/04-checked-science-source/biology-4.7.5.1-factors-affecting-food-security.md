## higher

Evaluate the relative significance of different threats to food security using data. Analyse the environmental impact and ethical dimensions of different solutions. Assess the trade-offs between food production efficiency and biodiversity conservation. Evaluate global distribution of food insecurity and the role of political, economic and biological factors.

## common_mistake

Food security is about HAVING ENOUGH food — not just producing food. Distribution and access are equally important. Even when enough food is produced globally, millions go hungry due to unequal distribution, poverty and conflict.

## key_note

Food security: enough food for all. Threats: rising population, changing diets (more meat), new pests/pathogens, climate change, conflict. Solutions: high-yield varieties, GM crops, better irrigation, reducing waste, changing diets, fair distribution.

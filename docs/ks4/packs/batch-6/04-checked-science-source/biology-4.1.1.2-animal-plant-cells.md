## common_mistake

NOT all plant cells have chloroplasts — ONLY cells exposed to light. Root cells are underground so never receive light and have NO chloroplasts. Students also often forget that plant cells have a cell wall AND a cell membrane — both are present. The cell wall is outside, the cell membrane is inside it.

## key_note

Animal cells: nucleus, cell membrane, cytoplasm, mitochondria, ribosomes. Plant cells: all of the above PLUS cell wall (cellulose), chloroplasts (light-exposed cells only), permanent vacuole.

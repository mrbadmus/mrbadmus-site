## higher

Explain why extremely high temperatures are needed for fusion in terms of Coulomb repulsion between nuclei. Describe the specific plasma confinement methods (magnetic/laser). Evaluate the advantages of fusion over fission as a future energy source using quantitative data where available.

## common_mistake

Fusion releases energy because the product nucleus has LESS MASS than the sum of the reactants — the mass defect converts to energy (E=mc²). Same principle as fission but with different nuclei. Fusion requires EXTREMELY HIGH TEMPERATURE (not just high pressure) because nuclei must have enough kinetic energy to overcome electrostatic repulsion.

## key_note

Fusion: two small nuclei join → larger nucleus + energy. Powers the Sun. D-T fusion: ²H + ³H → ⁴He + n. Requires ~100 million °C plasma. Confinement: magnetic (tokamak/ITER) or laser. Advantages: abundant fuel, clean waste, inherently safe. Challenge: net energy gain not yet achieved. Compare with fission: fusion cleaner but not yet practical.

## common_mistake

In beta decay, the MASS NUMBER stays the same — a neutron turns into a proton plus an electron. The atomic number INCREASES by 1 (one more proton). Students often think beta decay is like alpha and reduces mass number.

## key_note

Nuclear equations: mass numbers and atomic numbers must balance on both sides. Alpha (⁴₂He): A−4, Z−2. Beta (⁰₋₁e): A same, Z+1. Gamma (γ): no change. New element formed in alpha and beta (different Z). Always verify balance.

## fifas — CFIFA form (Convert step added; the four FIFA steps below are verbatim)

**Question:** Write the nuclear equation for alpha decay of ²²⁶₈₈Ra (radium).

**Convert:** [NEW — examined ✓] Nothing to convert — A and Z are dimensionless counts (mass number, atomic number), not measured quantities with units.

**F:** Alpha decay: A decreases by 4, Z decreases by 2. Product + ⁴₂He

**I:** A = 226 − 4 = 222; Z = 88 − 2 = 86. Z=86 is radon (Rn)

**F:** ²²⁶₈₈Ra → ²²²₈₆Rn + ⁴₂He

**A:** ²²⁶₈₈Ra → ²²²₈₆Rn + ⁴₂He  (check: 226 = 222+4 ✓; 88 = 86+2 ✓)

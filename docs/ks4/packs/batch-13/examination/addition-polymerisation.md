## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**: drawing the polymer with the C=C still present; leaving the side group off or moving it; forgetting the bonds through the brackets or the n; drawing a three-carbon backbone repeat unit for propene (the CH₃ goes on as a side group); thinking the repeat unit has fewer atoms than the monomer; "addition polymerisation gives off water".

**Command words**: Draw (the repeating unit / the monomer), Complete the equation, Give the name of the polymer, Explain why the atom economy is 100%.

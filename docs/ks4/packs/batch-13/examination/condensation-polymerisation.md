For example: ethanediol [displayed] and hexanedioic acid [displayed] polymerise to produce a polyester: [repeat-unit diagram]. Students should be able to explain the basic principles of condensation polymerisation by reference to the functional groups in the monomers and the repeating units in the polymers."

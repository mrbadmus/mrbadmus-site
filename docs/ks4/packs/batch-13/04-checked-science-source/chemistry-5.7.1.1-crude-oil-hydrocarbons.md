## higher

Write and balance combustion equations for any alkane. Calculate molar quantities from combustion equations. Evaluate environmental impact of fossil fuels: CO₂ → climate change; CO and particulates → local health effects. Compare energy content of different fuels.

## common_mistake

Alkanes are SATURATED — they have only SINGLE bonds and do NOT decolourise bromine water. ALKENES are UNSATURATED (have a C=C double bond) and DO decolourise bromine water. The bromine water test distinguishes saturated (no change) from unsaturated (decolourises).

## key_note

Crude oil: mixture of hydrocarbons (C and H only). Alkanes: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂, saturated (single bonds only), relatively unreactive. Complete combustion: CO₂ + H₂O. Incomplete combustion: CO (toxic) + soot. Crude oil is both fuel and feedstock.

## higher

Describe condensation polymerisation of amino acids to form proteins (peptide bonds, water lost). Explain DNA as a condensation polymer of nucleotides. Distinguish addition from condensation polymerisation using examples. Explain why condensation polymers are potentially biodegradable.

## common_mistake

DNA is a CONDENSATION polymer — nucleotides join with loss of water. This is different from addition polymers where no by-product forms. Starch and cellulose both have glucose as a monomer but have DIFFERENT structures and properties due to different types of linkage between the glucose units.

## key_note

DNA: condensation polymer of nucleotide monomers, double helix, encodes genetic information. Proteins: condensation polymers of amino acids. Starch/cellulose: glucose monomers, different linkages → different properties. Natural rubber: addition polymer of isoprene. Natural polymers usually biodegradable; synthetic often not.

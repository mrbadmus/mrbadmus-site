## higher

Link each test to its chemistry: CO₂ + Ca(OH)₂ → CaCO₃ + H₂O (ionic equation: Ca²⁺ + CO₃²⁻ → CaCO₃). Cl₂ + H₂O → HCl + HClO (bleaching mechanism). 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O (combustion — squeaky pop). Use test results in conjunction with other evidence to identify unknowns.

## common_mistake

Students confuse the splint tests. LIT splint → H₂ (squeaky pop). GLOWING splint → O₂ (relights). The litmus paper for chlorine MUST be DAMP — dry litmus paper will NOT bleach. Also: limewater turns milky with CO₂, not just any acidic gas.

## key_note

H₂: lit splint → squeaky pop. O₂: glowing splint → relights. CO₂: bubble through limewater → turns milky (CaCO₃ precipitate). Cl₂: damp litmus paper → bleaches white. All are simple, reliable tests for these four common gases.

## summary

Describe phytomining and bioleaching as alternatives to traditional metal extraction.

## common_mistake

In phytomining, plants are BURNED after harvesting — the metal is in the ASH, not extracted directly from the plants. In bioleaching, bacteria produce a SOLUTION (leachate) containing dissolved metal ions — the metal must then be recovered from this solution, e.g. by displacement or electrolysis.

## key_note

Phytomining: hyperaccumulator plants absorb metal ions → harvest and burn → metal in ash. Bioleaching: bacteria oxidise metal sulfide ores → metal ions leach into acidic solution → recover by displacement or electrolysis. Both: slow but lower impact, work on low-grade ores. Bioleaching produces acidic waste.

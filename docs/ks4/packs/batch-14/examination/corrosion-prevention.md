## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking:** "iron rusts in air alone"; "aluminium doesn't corrode because it's unreactive"; galvanising described as barrier only; "zinc is less reactive so it lasts longer"; sacrificial protection described without naming relative reactivity; painting and electroplating confused.
**Typical questions** ⚑ examiner-drafted
- *Three tubes: nail in water open to air; nail in boiled water under oil; nail in air with anhydrous calcium chloride. Which nail rusts? Explain. [3]* — the first (1); the second has no air/oxygen (1); the third has no water (1).
- *Explain why zinc protects iron even when the zinc coating is scratched. [2]* — zinc is more reactive than iron (1); zinc corrodes/is oxidised instead of iron (1).
- *Give one reason aluminium window frames do not need painting. [1]* — protective oxide layer.

## 6. Verdict
SOURCE HAS ERRORS — one wrong theory line (C8: a more reactive metal *speeds* rusting) that contradicts the lesson's own sacrificial protection; re-cut it. The spec's experiment (air AND water) is missing and is written in. Both quiz items usable on both routes. Nothing for Mide.

The properties of polymers depend on what monomers they are made from and the conditions under which they are made. ## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking:** "thermosetting polymers set when heated, so they melt first"; cross-links described as "strong intermolecular forces" (they are covalent bonds); "LDPE and HDPE are made from different monomers"; "a composite is just a mixture"; matrix and reinforcement swapped; "borosilicate is stronger" for "melts at a higher temperature".
**Typical questions** ⚑ examiner-drafted
- *Explain how LD and HD poly(ethene) can both be made from ethene. [2]* — same monomer, ethene (1); different reaction conditions, e.g. pressure/temperature/catalyst (1).
- *Explain, in terms of structure, why a thermosetting polymer does not melt when heated. [2]* — cross-links between chains (1); strong (covalent) bonds that are not broken by heating / chains cannot move apart (1).
- *Use the table (density, strength, cost, max temperature) to choose a material for a racing bike frame. Justify. [3]*

## common_mistake

The nucleus contains protons AND neutrons — NOT electrons. Electrons orbit the nucleus in shells. The mass of an atom comes almost entirely from the nucleus (protons + neutrons) — electron mass is negligible.

## key_note

Atom: nucleus (protons + neutrons) + electrons in shells. Proton: +1, mass 1. Neutron: 0, mass 1. Electron: −1, negligible mass. Protons = electrons in neutral atom. Nuclear radius ≈ 1/10,000 atomic radius — mostly empty space. Shells: 2, 8, 8...

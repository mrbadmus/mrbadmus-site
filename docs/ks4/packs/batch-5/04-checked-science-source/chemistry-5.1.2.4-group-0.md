## common_mistake

Noble gases are MONATOMIC — they exist as single atoms (He, Ne, Ar) — NOT as molecules (not He₂ or Ne₂). This is because they are already stable with full outer shells and have no reason to form bonds. Other non-metals like oxygen (O₂) and nitrogen (N₂) exist as diatomic molecules because they share electrons to achieve full outer shells.

## key_note

Noble gases: Group 0, full outer shells, extremely unreactive, monatomic. Boiling point increases down group (bigger atoms, stronger London dispersion forces). Helium: 2 outer electrons (full 1st shell). All others: 8 outer electrons (full outer shell).

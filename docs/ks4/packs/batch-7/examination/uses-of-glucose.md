## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**
- "Starch is stored because it is easier to digest / has more energy" — credited reason: insoluble, so no effect on osmosis.
- "Plants get protein / nitrogen from the air or from glucose alone" — nitrate ions from the soil.
- "Glucose is stored as glycogen in plants" — glycogen is animal/fungal; plants store starch.
- "Cellulose is stored for energy" — it strengthens the cell wall.
- "Plants make glucose for energy only" — also cellulose, starch, fats/oils, amino acids.
- "Starch is transported in the phloem" — sugars (sucrose) are; starch is the insoluble store.

**Command words**: Give, Name, Describe, Explain (why starch, why nitrate).

**Typical questions** ⚑ examiner-drafted
- *Give two uses of the glucose made in photosynthesis. [2]* — any two of: respiration; starch for storage; fats/oils for storage; cellulose for cell walls; amino acids/protein.
- *Explain why plants store glucose as starch. [2]* — starch is insoluble (1); so it does not affect water movement by osmosis (1).
- *A plant is grown in soil lacking nitrate ions. Explain why it grows poorly. [2]* — nitrate needed to make amino acids/proteins (1); proteins needed for growth (1).
- *Name the substance made from glucose that strengthens plant cell walls. [1]* — cellulose.
- *Describe how to test a leaf extract for starch. [2]* — iodine solution (1); orange-brown to blue-black (1). (Food tests are 4.2.2.1 RP; link only.)

**Required practical**: none. (AT 8 food tests are the 4.2.2.1 RP — 8464 RP3 / 8461 RP4 — not this lesson's.)

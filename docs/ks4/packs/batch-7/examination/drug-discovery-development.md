## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**: "drugs are tested on patients first"; "the placebo is a weaker dose"; "double-blind = patient doesn't know" (that is single-blind); "trials check it works" with no mention of toxicity or dose; "animals are tested so it is definitely safe for humans"; peer review confused with the trial itself.

**Command words**: Describe the stages (preclinical in cells/tissues/animals → clinical in healthy volunteers at very low dose → patients to find optimum dose → double-blind with placebo) [4–6]; Explain why a placebo / double-blind is used [2]; Give one reason results are peer reviewed [1].

**Required practical**: none.

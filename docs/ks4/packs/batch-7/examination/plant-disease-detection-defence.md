## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**: "plants make antibodies"; "magnesium is needed for growth / nitrate makes chlorophyll" (swapped — nitrate → protein → growth; magnesium → chlorophyll); "chlorosis = disease caused by a pathogen" (here it is magnesium deficiency); bark "protects the tree" with no mention of dead cells falling off; thorns given as a defence against microorganisms; detection and identification confused (symptoms detect; manual / lab / monoclonal kit identify).

**Command words**: Describe physical and chemical defences [4]; Explain why a magnesium-deficient plant has yellow leaves [2] (less chlorophyll → less photosynthesis); Explain why a nitrate-deficient plant is stunted [2] (less protein → less growth); (HT) Describe how a gardener could identify the disease [2–3].

**Required practical**: none.

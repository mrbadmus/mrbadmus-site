## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**
- Oxygen written as a reactant, or the equation reversed (respiration written instead).
- "Plants photosynthesise instead of respiring" / "plants only respire at night".
- "Plants get their food from the soil" — mass comes from CO₂ (and water), not soil.
- "Light" written as a reactant/substance rather than the energy source over the arrow.
- Glucose formula miswritten (C6H12O6 confused with CO2/H2O roles).
- Endothermic/exothermic swapped.

**Command words**: Complete (the equation), Name, Give, Describe, Explain.

**Typical questions** ⚑ examiner-drafted
- *Complete the word equation for photosynthesis: carbon dioxide + ____ → ____ + oxygen [2]* — water (1); glucose (1).
- *Photosynthesis is an endothermic reaction. What does this mean? [1]* — energy is transferred from the surroundings (to the chloroplasts) / energy is taken in.
- *Name the sub-cellular structure where photosynthesis takes place. [1]* — chloroplast (not chlorophyll).
- *Give the chemical symbol for glucose. [1]* — C6H12O6.
- *Explain why root hair cells do not carry out photosynthesis. [2]* — no chloroplasts (1); no light reaches them underground (1).

**Required practical**: none in this lesson (RP is 4.4.1.2 — owned by rate-of-photosynthesis).

**Equations**: chemical equation only (word = must know; symbols = recognise). No calculation.

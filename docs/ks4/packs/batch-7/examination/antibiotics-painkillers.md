## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**: "antibiotics kill viruses / cure a cold"; "the patient becomes resistant" (the bacteria do); "bacteria become resistant because of the antibiotic" (the mutation was already there; the antibiotic selects it); "painkillers cure the illness"; "antibiotics kill all pathogens".

**Command words**: Explain why antibiotics are not prescribed for a cold [2]; Explain why the patient should finish the course [2]; Give one reason it is hard to develop antiviral drugs [1] (must damage body tissues/cells).

**Required practical**: none on this page. RP2 (8461, biology only — effect of antiseptics/antibiotics on bacterial growth) belongs to `culturing-microorganisms`; a link is fine.

## common_mistake

Photosynthesis only happens in LIGHT and only in cells with CHLOROPLASTS. Respiration happens ALL THE TIME in ALL living cells. Never say 'plants don't respire' — they do, constantly. In bright light they photosynthesise faster than they respire, which is why we see net O₂ production.

## key_note

Photosynthesis: CO₂ + H₂O → glucose + O₂. Needs light energy. Occurs in chloroplasts. Endothermic — stores energy. Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light, reflects green.

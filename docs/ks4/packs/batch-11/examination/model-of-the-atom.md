Spec statements (verbatim, 8464 5.1.1.3 = 8462 4.1.1.3):
- "New experimental evidence may lead to a scientific model being changed or replaced."
- "Before the discovery of the electron, atoms were thought to be tiny spheres that could not be divided."
- "The discovery of the electron led to the plum pudding model of the atom. The plum pudding model suggested that the atom is a ball of positive charge with negative electrons embedded in it."
- "The results from the alpha particle scattering experiment led to the conclusion that the mass of an atom was concentrated at the centre (nucleus) and that the nucleus was charged. This nuclear model replaced the plum pudding model."
- "Niels Bohr adapted the nuclear model by suggesting that electrons orbit the nucleus at specific distances. The theoretical calculations of Bohr agreed with experimental observations."
- "Later experiments led to the idea that the positive charge of any nucleus could be subdivided into a whole number of smaller particles, each particle having the same amount of positive charge. The name proton was given to these particles."
- "The experimental work of James Chadwick provided the evidence to show the existence of neutrons within the nucleus. This was about 20 years after the nucleus became an accepted scientific idea."
- "Students should be able to describe: • why the new evidence from the scattering experiment led to a change in the atomic model • the difference between the plum pudding model of the atom and the nuclear model of the atom."
- "Details of experimental work supporting the Bohr model are not required. Details of Chadwick's experimental work are not required."

## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**
- "Most alpha particles bounced back." Most went straight through.
- "The ones that bounced back hit electrons." They were repelled by a small positive nucleus that holds most of the mass.
- Describing the difference between plum pudding and nuclear models by only one feature. Full marks need two: where the positive charge and mass are (spread out vs concentrated in a nucleus) and empty space (none vs mostly empty space).
- "Plum pudding had no electrons." It was built around the electron.
- "Bohr discovered the neutron." Chadwick did, about 20 years after the nucleus was accepted.

**Command words**: Describe (the difference between…), Explain why the model changed, Give one observation… and what it shows.

**Typical questions** ⚑ examiner-drafted
- *Describe the difference between the plum pudding model and the nuclear model of the atom. [3]*: nuclear model has a nucleus / plum pudding has none (1); positive charge (and mass) concentrated at the centre vs spread throughout (1); nuclear atom mostly empty space (1).
- *Explain why the results of the alpha scattering experiment led to the plum pudding model being replaced. [2]*: some alpha particles deflected through large angles / bounced back (1); which plum pudding could not explain, so mass and positive charge must be concentrated in a small nucleus (1).
- *Which scientist provided evidence for the neutron? [1]*: Chadwick.

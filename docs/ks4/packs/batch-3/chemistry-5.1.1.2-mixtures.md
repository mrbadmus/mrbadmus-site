## common_mistake

Filtration only removes INSOLUBLE solids. You CANNOT filter salt from water — salt is dissolved (soluble). Use evaporation or distillation instead. Also: in distillation you collect the VAPOUR that condenses — the liquid. Crystallisation collects the SOLID that forms as the solution cools.

## key_note

Filtration: insoluble solid from liquid. Crystallisation: dissolved solid from solution (cool slowly). Simple distillation: liquid from solution. Fractional distillation: liquids with different boiling points. Chromatography: dissolved substances — Rf = substance distance ÷ solvent distance.

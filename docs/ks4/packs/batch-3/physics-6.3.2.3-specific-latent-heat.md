## common_mistake

During a change of state, temperature does NOT change — the flat sections on a heating curve. E = mL has NO temperature change term — unlike ΔE = mcΔθ. Students often confuse the two equations. If temperature is changing, use ΔE = mcΔθ. If state is changing (temperature constant), use E = mL.

## key_note

E = mL. No temperature change during state change — energy goes into PE (breaking bonds). Fusion: solid ↔ liquid. Vaporisation: liquid ↔ gas. Lv > Lf (more energy to boil than melt). Water Lf = 334 kJ/kg; Lv = 2260 kJ/kg. Steam burns worse than boiling water — releases latent heat on condensing.

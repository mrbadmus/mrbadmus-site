## higher

Calculate theoretical yield using moles. Atom economy = (Mr desired products ÷ Mr all products) × 100. Addition reactions have 100% atom economy. High atom economy = less waste, more sustainable, more economical. Evaluate different reaction routes on the basis of atom economy.

## common_mistake

Never change the SUBSCRIPT numbers in a formula when balancing — only add or change the large numbers (coefficients) IN FRONT of formulas. Changing H₂O to H₂O₂ would create a different compound (hydrogen peroxide, not water). Always balance by adjusting coefficients only.

## key_note

Conservation of mass: atoms are rearranged, never created or destroyed → total mass reactants = total mass products. Balance equations by adjusting coefficients only — never change chemical formulae. Check all atoms balance on both sides.

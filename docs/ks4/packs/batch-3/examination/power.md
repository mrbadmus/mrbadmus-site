## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**
- Time left in minutes or hours (q1 is the archetype); kW not converted to W when energy is wanted in J.
- Power confused with energy or force ("a more powerful motor lifts more weight").
- "Takes longer so works harder" — longer time = more power (q2 option 3).
- Climbing problems: using mass (kg) instead of weight (N) as the force in W = Fs; omitting g.
- Unit errors: "W" read as work vs watt; power given in J or J/s written as "Js".
- Rearranging P = E/t as E = P/t.

**Command words**: Calculate, Write down the equation that links… (power, energy transferred, time — both P equations are on the recall list), Define, Compare, Explain, Give an example.

**Typical questions** ⚑ examiner-drafted
- *Write down the equation that links energy transferred, power and time. [1]* — P = E/t (accept E = Pt, words).
- *Define power. [1]* — the rate of energy transfer / rate of doing work (accept energy transferred per second).
- *A 1.2 kW microwave is used for 2.5 minutes. Calculate the energy transferred. [3]* — 1200 W and 150 s (1); E = 1200 × 150 (1); = 180,000 J (1).
- *A crane lifts a 600 kg load through 12 m in 40 s. g = 9.8 N/kg. Calculate the useful power output. [4]* — Ep = 600 × 9.8 × 12 (1) = 70,560 J (1); P = 70,560 ÷ 40 (1); = 1764 W (≈ 1.8 kW) (1).
- *Two motors lift identical loads through the same height. Motor X takes 6 s; motor Y takes 9 s. Explain which motor has the greater power. [2]* — X (1); same work done / same energy transferred in less time, so a greater rate of energy transfer (1).
- 6-mark extended responses do not arise from 6.1.1.4 alone; power appears inside efficiency and energy-resources 6-markers.

**Required practical**: none. (Measuring personal power by stair-running is a common class activity, not an AQA RP.)

**Equations**: P = E/t and P = W/t — **learn it** per the spec's **recall list** (8463 Appendix A eqs 12, 13; "recall and apply both equations"); **but both are printed on the June 2026 8463 and 8464/8465 sheets**. E = Pt — recall list (eq 21), also printed on both June 2026 sheets. Supporting Ep = mgh — recall list (eq 11), printed on both June 2026 sheets. The chip should follow whichever authority the site has adopted for "Equation sheet" vs "Learn it" (see §6) — the spec and the 2026 sheet disagree for every equation in this lesson.

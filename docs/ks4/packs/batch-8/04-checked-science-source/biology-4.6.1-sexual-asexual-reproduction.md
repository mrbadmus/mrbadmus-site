## summary

Compare sexual and asexual reproduction and explain the role of meiosis in sexual reproduction.

## higher

Meiosis produces four haploid genetically different gametes from one diploid cell. Key events: homologous chromosomes pair and exchange segments (CROSSING OVER) in prophase I; homologous pairs separate in meiosis I; chromatids separate in meiosis II. Independent assortment (random orientation of chromosome pairs) and crossing over both increase genetic variation. The combination of meiosis and fertilisation produces enormous genetic diversity.

## common_mistake

MITOSIS produces IDENTICAL cells (used for growth and repair). MEIOSIS produces GAMETES with HALF the chromosome number — and they are genetically different from each other. Students often confuse these two. Remember: MEiosis → gamEtes. Mitosis → most body cells.

## key_note

Asexual: 1 parent, mitosis, clones, fast, no variation. Sexual: 2 parents, meiosis + fertilisation, variation, drives evolution. Meiosis halves chromosome number: 46 → 23. Fertilisation restores: 23 + 23 = 46.

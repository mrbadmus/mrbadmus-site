## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**
- Short-sight and long-sight named the wrong way round ("short-sighted = can't see short distances").
- Concave/convex swapped; "concave makes light converge".
- "Glasses change the shape of the eye's lens."
- Ray diagrams drawn with the image focused *on* the retina for the uncorrected eye, or rays bending at the retina.
- "Laser surgery reshapes the lens" — it reshapes the cornea.

**Command words**: Describe, Explain, Interpret (ray diagram), Complete the ray diagram, Evaluate (methods of correction).

**Typical questions** ⚑ examiner-drafted
- *The diagram shows rays focusing in front of the retina. Name the defect and the type of lens used to correct it. [2]* — myopia / short-sight (1); concave / diverging lens (1).
- *Complete the diagram to show how a convex lens corrects hyperopia. [2]* — rays converge before entering the eye (1); focus on the retina (1).
- *Evaluate the use of laser surgery rather than spectacles to correct short-sight. [4]* — e.g. no need for glasses / permanent / convenient vs. risk of complications / cost / not reversible; judgement.

**Required practical**: none.
**Equations**: none.

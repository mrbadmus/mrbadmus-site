## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**
- Cerebellum and cerebrum/cortex confused (the names are close).
- Medulla credited with "reflexes" generally, or with thinking; it handles unconscious activities.
- "MRI shows electrical activity" — MRI shows structure/blood flow; electrical activity is EEG (context).
- "Brain damage can't be studied because you can't experiment on people" — the spec's answer is that patients with existing damage are studied.

**Command words**: Identify (on a diagram), Describe, Explain (HT), Evaluate (HT, WS 1.5), Suggest.

**Typical questions** ⚑ examiner-drafted
- *Label the cerebral cortex, cerebellum and medulla on the diagram. [3]*
- *A person has damage to their medulla. Suggest one function that may be affected. [1]* — heart rate / breathing rate.
- (HT) *Explain why it is difficult to treat brain damage. [2]* — brain is complex / delicate (1); damage to surrounding tissue / neurones not easily replaced / drugs may not reach the brain (1).
- (HT) *Give two methods scientists use to map brain function. [2]* — study patients with brain damage; electrically stimulate regions; MRI.

## 1. Energy Stores and Systems (`energy-stores-systems`)

# Flags — energy-stores-systems

**ENERGY-STORES-SYSTEMS-F1** · WRONG · theory 3 "Kettle: chemical store → thermal store (water + surroundings) via heating and electrical work." · An electric kettle has no chemical store. This is the spec's own example. · Energy is transferred electrically (work done when a current flows) to the thermal store of the heating element, then by heating to the thermal store of the water; some goes to the thermal store of the kettle and surroundings. · 8464 6.1.1.1 / 8463 4.1.1.1 ("bringing water to a boil in an electric kettle") · Rewrite the example as above. The theory text is re-cuttable.

**ENERGY-STORES-SYSTEMS-F2** · WRONG · q2 wx2 "Chemical energy is a store — energy held in molecular bonds, released when bonds break in reactions." · Breaking bonds takes in energy. Energy is released when new bonds form. · "energy must be supplied to break bonds in the reactants; energy is released when bonds in the products are formed." · 8464 5.5.1.3 / 8462 4.5.1.3 (HT only) · **Do not use q2 as written.** Write a replacement store-vs-pathway item. Never say "released when bonds break".

**ENERGY-STORES-SYSTEMS-F3** · GAP · The spec's five common situations: only "projected upwards" is taught; "hitting an obstacle" appears only in passing; "accelerated by a constant force" and "vehicle slowing down" are missing. · Pupils are asked to describe every store change in these situations. · The store changes for all five are written in the source file's new section "Spec core missing from the frozen data". · 8464 6.1.1.1 / 8463 4.1.1.1 · Teach all five situations, each as before store → pathway → after store.

**ENERGY-STORES-SYSTEMS-F4** · IMPRECISE · theory 3 "HEATING — … conduction, convection or radiation" and "RADIATION — … (light, infrared, sound etc.)"; theory 1 "chemical store → thermal store + light radiation"; matching "Radiation — light, infrared or sound waves". · Infrared is listed under two pathways. Sound is a mechanical wave, not radiation. Light is a pathway, not a store. · Pathways: mechanically (a force does work), electrically (a current does work), by heating, by radiation/waves. Energy carried away by light or sound ends in the thermal store of the surroundings. · 8464 6.1.1.1 · Give each pathway one meaning. Never show light or sound as a store.

**ENERGY-STORES-SYSTEMS-F5** · IMPRECISE (minor) · theory 1 "the TOTAL energy always stays the same" · Only true of a closed system. · "no net change to the total energy" in a closed system · 8464 6.1.2.1 / 8463 4.1.2.1 · Say "in a closed system".

## 15. Changes of State (`changes-of-state`)

# Flags — changes-of-state

**CHANGES-OF-STATE-F1** · IMPRECISE · theory 3 "DURING BOILING: Energy supplied → particles gain enough energy to completely overcome intermolecular forces → escape from liquid surface into gas phase." · That describes evaporation. Boiling happens throughout the liquid at the boiling point (bubbles of vapour form inside it). Evaporation is from the surface, at any temperature. The spec names both. · "(melt, freeze, boil, evaporate, condense or sublimate)" · 8464 6.3.1.2 / 8463 4.3.1.2 · Distinguish them: evaporation = surface, below the boiling point; boiling = throughout, at the boiling point.

**CHANGES-OF-STATE-F2** · IMPRECISE · theory 2 "No new chemical bonds are formed or broken between different types of molecule." · Muddled. It does not say what changes and what doesn't. · Nothing inside the particles changes; only the forces between particles are overcome (or re-form), so only the arrangement changes. The spec's test: "the material recovers its original properties if the change is reversed." · 8464 6.3.1.2 / 8463 4.3.1.2 · Use the spec's test as the definition.

**CHANGES-OF-STATE-F3** · IMPRECISE · theory 3 "SOLID: … Strong intermolecular forces … LIQUID: … Weaker intermolecular forces than solid." · The forces do not change in kind on melting. In a liquid the particles have enough energy to move past each other while still attracting each other. "Intermolecular" only fits molecular substances; metals and salts melt too. · — · 8464 6.3.1.1 / 8463 4.3.1.1 · Say "forces of attraction between particles"; explain liquids by the particles' energy, not by weaker forces.

**CHANGES-OF-STATE-F4** · IMPRECISE (minor) · q1 wx1 "In a sealed container with no evaporation, mass is perfectly conserved — but even with some evaporation, the PRINCIPLE is conservation of mass in a change of state." · If water did escape as vapour, the ice would weigh less. The point is that freezing itself loses no mass. Key right; wx1 aligned with option 1. · — · 8464 6.3.1.2 · q1 is usable as written. Design's reveal can say "freezing doesn't change the mass; only water escaping would".

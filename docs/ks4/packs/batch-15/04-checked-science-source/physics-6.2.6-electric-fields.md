## higher

Represent electric fields using field line diagrams for single charges, pairs of charges, and parallel plates. Explain the uniform field between parallel plates. Calculate electric field strength: E = F/q. Describe how field strength varies with distance from a point charge. Link electric field to potential difference.

## common_mistake

Electric field lines go from POSITIVE to NEGATIVE — the direction a positive charge would move. Don't confuse with magnetic field lines which go from N to S. A uniform field (between parallel plates) has equal spacing — field strength is the same at all points.

## key_note

Electric field: force per unit charge. Field lines: + to −, closer = stronger. Patterns: single charges radiate; opposite charges arc between them; uniform field between parallel plates. E = F/q. Sparks: air ionised in strong fields. Lightning conductor: provides safe discharge path.

## summary

Define density and calculate it from mass and volume for solids and liquids.

## common_mistake

Units for density are kg/m³ (SI) or g/cm³ (also common). Do NOT mix — if mass is in grams and volume in cm³, density comes out in g/cm³. Convert to kg/m³ by multiplying by 1000. Also: 1 cm³ = 1 × 10⁻⁶ m³.

## key_note

ρ = m/V. Units: kg/m³. Denser = more mass per volume. Solids > liquids > gases (usually). Ice less dense than water (exception). Float if ρ_object < ρ_fluid. RP17: regular solids — measure dimensions; irregular solids — displacement method.

## fifas — CFIFA form (Convert step added; the four FIFA steps below are verbatim)

**Question:** A block has mass 540 g and volume 200 cm³. Calculate its density in g/cm³.

**Convert:** [NEW — examined ✓] Nothing to convert — mass is given in g and volume in cm³, which already match the g/cm³ the question asks for; converting to kg and m³ would be needed only if the answer were wanted in kg/m³.

**F:** ρ = m ÷ V

**I:** m = 540 g, V = 200 cm³

**F:** ρ = 540 ÷ 200

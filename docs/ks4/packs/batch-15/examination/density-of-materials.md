Chained calculations (rule 3) the lesson must teach:
- RP regular solid: Step 1 V = l × w × h (or πr²h), Step 2 ρ = m ÷ V. Yes, chains.
- RP irregular solid: Step 1 V = reading after − reading before (1 ml = 1 cm³), Step 2 ρ = m ÷ V. Yes (a subtraction then the equation).
- Mass conserved through a change of state (the spec's "apply … to changes where mass is conserved"): Step 1 m = ρ₁ × V₁, Step 2 V₂ = m ÷ ρ₂. Yes, chains; not in the source.
- Single-step ρ = m/V and its rearrangements: no chain.

## 5. Verdict
SOURCE HAS ERRORS. The science, values and arithmetic are correct, and q1 is usable on all four routes. q2 has a distractor that is also right, so it is not usable as written. The RP is labelled with the Combined number only. The source never converts g/cm³ ↔ kg/m³ in a worked example and never applies ρ = m/V to a mass-conserved change, which the spec names; both are chained or converted calculations that need their own worked examples.

## common_mistake

TRANSPIRATION is the loss of water vapour from PLANT LEAVES through stomata — it is NOT the same as evaporation from the soil or water surface. Both contribute to atmospheric water, but transpiration is specifically the biological process in plants. Also: precipitation is NOT just rain — it includes snow, sleet and hail.

## key_note

Water cycle: evaporation + transpiration → condensation (clouds) → precipitation (rain/snow) → runoff/groundwater → back to sea. Plants contribute via transpiration. Deforestation reduces transpiration → less rainfall. Key: water is continuously recycled.

## Spec core missing from the frozen data (written from the spec, examiner)

8464/8461 4.7.2.2, verbatim: "explain the importance of the carbon and water cycles to living organisms." … "The water cycle provides fresh water for plants and animals on land before draining into the seas. The frozen data never says the cycle supplies **fresh** water on land. Teach: when sea water evaporates, the dissolved salts are left behind, so the vapour, and the precipitation it becomes, is fresh water that land plants and animals can use, before it drains back into the sea.

## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**: useful ÷ wasted; efficiency > 1 not noticed; % and decimal mixed (0.35% written for 35%); efficiency given a unit (J); kJ and J mixed in the ratio; wasted energy "destroyed" or "used up"; "a more efficient device uses less energy" (it wastes less per useful joule).

**Command words**: Calculate, Write down the equation, Give, Describe (HT: ways to increase efficiency), Explain (why a Sankey arrow is wider), Compare.

**Required practical**: none.

**Equations**: both efficiency equations — spec recall list (8463 App. A eqs 14, 15), **printed on both June 2026 sheets** → chip "On the sheet".

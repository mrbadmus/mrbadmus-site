Spec statement (verbatim, the whole of 4.7.3.1; 8464 = 8461): "Biodiversity is the variety of all the different species of organisms on earth, or within an ecosystem. A great biodiversity ensures the stability of ecosystems by reducing the dependence of one species on another for food, shelter and the maintenance of the physical environment. The future of the human species on Earth relies on us maintaining a good level of biodiversity. Many human activities are reducing biodiversity and only recently have measures been taken to try to stop this reduction." Skills: "WS 1.4 Explain how waste, deforestation and global warming have an impact on biodiversity."

**Definition conflict.** The source defines biodiversity as "the variety of life on Earth" with "two components: species diversity (number of species and relative abundance) and genetic diversity". AQA's definition is species-only: *variety of all the different species of organisms on earth, or within an ecosystem*. Relative abundance and genetic diversity are A-level framings. The source's common_mistake is built on relative abundance and contradicts the AQA definition. This is not a conflict between AQA sources — it is the source against the spec, so the spec wins.

## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**: biodiversity = number of organisms/individuals; "variety of life" with no "species"; removing one species only affects that species; biodiversity matters for wildlife, not people; stability explained as "more animals" rather than less dependence on any one species.

**Typical questions**: *What is meant by biodiversity? [1]* — the variety of (all the different) species (of organisms) on Earth / in an ecosystem. *Explain why a high biodiversity makes an ecosystem more stable. [2]* — species depend less on any one other species (1) for food / shelter / maintaining the physical environment (1). *Explain how [deforestation / waste / global warming] reduces biodiversity. [2–4]* (WS 1.4). *Suggest why humans should maintain biodiversity.*

## 6. Verdict
SOURCE HAS ERRORS. The lesson's definition of biodiversity (theory, key_note, common_mistake) is not AQA's and would cost marks: AQA defines it as the variety of different **species** on Earth or in an ecosystem; relative abundance and genetic diversity are A-level framings, and the common_mistake states that a 100-species ecosystem can have "low biodiversity", which contradicts the spec. q2's wrong-answer explanation misdescribes the Svalbard vault. Neither frozen quiz item tests 4.7.3.1; the bank must be built from the spec. The `higher` field holds no HT content. The theory spends most of its length on 4.7.3.2–4.7.3.6, which have their own lessons.

**For Mide:** nothing. The definition is settled by the spec text (8464 = 8461 verbatim).

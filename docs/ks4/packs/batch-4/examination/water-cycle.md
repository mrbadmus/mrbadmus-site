## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**: clouds are water vapour/steam; rain is salty because it came from the sea; transpiration = plants "breathing" or respiring; precipitation = rain only; water is "used up" by organisms; arrows on a cycle diagram drawn the wrong way (e.g. condensation going up).

**Typical questions**: label/complete a water-cycle diagram (evaporation, transpiration, condensation, precipitation); explain why the water cycle is important to living organisms on land (provides fresh water; salts left behind on evaporation); explain how removing trees affects the cycle.

SOURCE OK WITH FLAGS. No science errors; both quiz items usable on all four routes. The spec reference on the site (4.7.3) is wrong — 4.7.2.2. The one thing the spec actually asks this lesson to say — the water cycle provides **fresh** water for plants and animals on land before it drains to the sea — is missing and must be taught.

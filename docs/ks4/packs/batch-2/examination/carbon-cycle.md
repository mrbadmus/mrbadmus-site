## 5. For the lesson author
**Misconceptions seen in AQA marking**
- "Plants take in CO₂ and do not give it out" (plants respire too).
- "Decomposers release CO₂ by decomposing" with no respiration — loses the mark that asks *how*.
- "Animals take in CO₂ when they eat" — carbon passes as carbon compounds in food (feeding), not as CO₂.
- Arrow direction errors on cycle diagrams (photosynthesis arrow pointing *to* the atmosphere).
- Confusing global warming with ozone depletion; "CO₂ makes a hole in the ozone layer".
- Deforestation "removes oxygen" (marks are for less CO₂ absorbed by photosynthesis, plus CO₂ released by burning/decay).
- "Fossil fuels release carbon" — answer must say carbon **dioxide** is released by **burning/combustion**.

**Command words**: Describe, Explain, Name / Give (process at arrow X), Complete (the diagram), Suggest, Evaluate (e.g. biofuels), Use the information.

**Typical questions** ⚑ examiner-drafted
- *Name processes A and B on the diagram. [2]* — A photosynthesis (1); B respiration / combustion / decay as appropriate (1).
- *Explain how carbon in a dead leaf is returned to the atmosphere. [3]* — microorganisms / decomposers / bacteria and fungi break down the leaf (1); they respire (1); releasing carbon dioxide (1).
- *Explain why deforestation increases the CO₂ concentration in the atmosphere. [4]* — fewer trees so less photosynthesis (1); so less CO₂ removed (1); trees burned (1) or decayed by microorganisms that respire (1), releasing CO₂. Max 4.
- 6-mark (levels of response): *Describe how a carbon atom in CO₂ in the air can become part of an animal and later return to the air.* Indicative content: photosynthesis by plants → glucose / carbon compounds; used to make biomass (e.g. proteins, cellulose); animal eats plant — carbon in food passes along food chain; animal respires → CO₂; or animal dies/produces waste; decomposers break down and respire → CO₂; (optional) fossilisation and combustion. L3 (5–6): full, logically sequenced route with processes named and linked; L2 (3–4): route largely correct, some processes named; L1 (1–2): isolated processes. ⚑ examiner-drafted.

## common_mistake

A COMPOUND is NOT a mixture. In a compound, elements are CHEMICALLY BONDED — you need a chemical reaction to separate them (e.g. electrolysis to split water into hydrogen and oxygen). In a MIXTURE, substances are just mixed — physical methods separate them. Water is a compound. Saltwater is a mixture.

## key_note

Element: one type of atom. Compound: two or more elements chemically bonded — new properties, chemical separation only. Mixture: not bonded — keeps own properties, physical separation possible. Equations must be balanced — atoms conserved.

## common_mistake

Enzymes are DENATURED at high temperatures — NOT killed. They are proteins, not living things. You cannot kill a protein. Denaturation means the active site shape is permanently changed. Cooling the enzyme down afterwards will NOT bring it back to life — the damage is permanent.

## key_note

Lock and key: enzyme active site + specific substrate → enzyme-substrate complex → products released → enzyme reused. Denaturation is permanent — caused by high temperature or extreme pH.

## rp

RP3 — Investigate the effect of pH on the rate of amylase activity. Use iodine solution to test for starch at regular intervals. Compare time taken to digest starch at different pH values.

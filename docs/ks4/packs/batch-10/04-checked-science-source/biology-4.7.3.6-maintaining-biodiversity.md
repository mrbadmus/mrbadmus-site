## summary

Describe the programmes humans use to reduce their impact on ecosystems and maintain biodiversity, and the conflicts these can involve.

## common_mistake

'Maintaining biodiversity' is about active human PROGRAMMES (breeding, protecting habitats, hedgerows, recycling, cutting emissions), not just 'stop polluting'. Also be ready to explain the CONFLICT: conservation costs money and land, so it competes with farming, building and jobs.

## key_note

Programmes to maintain biodiversity: breeding programmes for endangered species; protecting and regenerating rare habitats; reintroducing field margins and hedgerows; governments reducing deforestation and CO₂ emissions; recycling instead of landfill. These conflict with human needs (cost, food, land, livelihoods).

Metals and oxygen

Gaining oxygen is oxidation.

Metals react with oxygen to produce metal oxides. These are oxidation reactions because the metal gains oxygen. The reverse, losing oxygen, is reduction.

magnesium + oxygenmagnesium oxide magnesium is oxidised

Metal oxides are bases: they neutralise acids.

Higher · ionic equations and electrons

{{ route }}

Oxidation is the loss of electrons and reduction is the gain of electrons. Write displacement as an ionic equation: the sulfate ions do not change, so they are left out.

Fe + Cu2+Fe2+ + Cu
  • Each iron atom loses 2 electrons to become Fe2+: iron is oxidised.
  • Each Cu2+ ion gains 2 electrons to become a copper atom: Cu2+ is reduced.

Key fact

The reactivity of a metal is related to its tendency to form positive ions. A more reactive metal displaces a less reactive metal from its compounds.

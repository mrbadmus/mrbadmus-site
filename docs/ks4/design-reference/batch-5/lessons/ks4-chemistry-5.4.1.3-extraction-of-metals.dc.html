Extraction of metals and reduction

Where a metal sits compared with carbon decides how we get it out of its ore. Below carbon: heat with carbon, and the oxide is reduced. Above carbon: electrolysis.

Above or below carbon?

Carbon decides the method.

Most metals are found as compounds, in ores, and need chemical reactions to extract the metal. Metals less reactive than carbon can be extracted from their oxides by heating with carbon. Metals more reactive than carbon are extracted by electrolysis of a molten compound, which needs a lot of energy. Pick a metal and choose.

Reduced and oxidised

Lose oxygen: reduced. Gain oxygen: oxidised.

Reduction involves the loss of oxygen. When a metal oxide is heated with carbon, the oxide loses oxygen and is reduced; the carbon gains that oxygen and is oxidised. The worked example is first; then you tag the other two.

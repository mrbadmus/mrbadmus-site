Data cards · finite or renewable

Is it replaced as fast as we use it?

Humans use the Earth’s resources for warmth, shelter, food and transport. Natural resources, supplemented by agriculture, provide food, timber, clothing and fuels. Finite resources from the Earth, oceans and atmosphere are processed to provide energy and materials.

Renewable: replaced by natural processes as fast as it is used. Finite: not replaced on a human timescale, however much is left. Read each card, then decide.

Sustainable development

Development that meets the needs of current generations without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs. Chemistry plays an important role, improving agricultural and industrial processes to provide new products.

Recycling is not renewing

Recycling aluminium uses about 95% less energy than extracting new aluminium, and makes the ore last longer. The aluminium is still a finite resource. More in the recycling lesson.

Orders of magnitude · how long will it last?

Divide what is left by how much is used each year. Reserves and use must be in the same unit first. Then read the size of the answer: tens of years is urgent; millions of years is not.

years left = reserves ÷ use per year

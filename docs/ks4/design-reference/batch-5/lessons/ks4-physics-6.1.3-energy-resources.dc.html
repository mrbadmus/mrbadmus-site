Nine resources · two questions

Will it run out? Can you rely on it?

A renewable resource is one that is being, or can be, replenished as it is used. A non-renewable resource will run out. A reliable resource can be used whenever it is needed, or at times you can predict. Pick a card, then place it.

Describe, then explain

  • Coal fell from 35% to 2%: burning coal releases carbon dioxide, and the country chose to cut its emissions.
  • Wind and solar rose from 1% to 30%: they release no carbon dioxide while running.
  • Gas stayed large: it can be used on demand when the wind drops or it is dark.

What science can and can’t do

Science can identify environmental problems, such as the carbon dioxide from burning coal. It does not always have the power to deal with them: whether to close a power station, or build a wind farm, is also decided by political, social, ethical and economic considerations.

Key fact

Fossil fuels and nuclear fuel are non-renewable. Bio-fuel, wind, hydro-electricity, geothermal, the tides, the Sun and water waves are renewable. Every resource has some environmental impact.

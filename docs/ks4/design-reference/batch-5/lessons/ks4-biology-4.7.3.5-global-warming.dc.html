The cause, in brief

Carbon dioxide and methane are rising.

The levels of carbon dioxide and methane in the atmosphere are increasing, and this is contributing to global warming. Burning fossil fuels, cutting down forests and farming livestock all add to them. How these gases warm the Earth is in the chemistry lesson on greenhouse gases; this lesson is about what warming does to living things.

How sure are scientists?

Agreement from thousands of studies; uncertainty in the detail.

The consensus

The scientific consensus about global warming and climate change is based on systematic reviews of thousands of peer-reviewed publications.

The uncertainty

Climate is complex and predictions rely on models, so exactly how much and how fast each place will change is uncertain. The overall trend is not.

Key fact

Global warming changes the distribution of species, changes migration patterns, floods coastal habitats as sea levels rise, and can reduce biodiversity.

Before the experiment

From solid spheres to plum pudding.

Before the electron was discovered, atoms were thought to be tiny spheres that could not be divided. The discovery of the electron led to the plum pudding model: the atom is a ball of positive charge with negative electrons embedded in it.

Fire alpha particles at thin gold foil.

Key fact

Alpha scattering showed that the mass of an atom is concentrated at the centre, in a nucleus, and that the nucleus is charged. The nuclear model replaced the plum pudding model.

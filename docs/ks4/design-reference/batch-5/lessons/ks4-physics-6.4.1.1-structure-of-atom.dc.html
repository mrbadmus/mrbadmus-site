Zoom · to scale

The nucleus is tiny. The rest is empty space.

An atom has a radius of about 1 × 10−10 m. The radius of its nucleus is less than 1/10 000 of that, around 1 × 10−14 m. Zoom in until you can see it.

Energy levels

Absorb radiation, move up. Emit radiation, move down.

Electrons are arranged at different distances from the nucleus: different energy levels. An electron moves further from the nucleus (to a higher energy level) only when it absorbs electromagnetic radiation, and moves closer (to a lower level) by emitting it.

Key fact

An atom has a positively charged nucleus of protons and neutrons, with electrons around it. The nucleus has less than 1/10 000 of the atom’s radius but almost all of its mass.

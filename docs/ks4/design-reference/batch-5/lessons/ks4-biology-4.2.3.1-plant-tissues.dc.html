In the vein · xylem and phloem

Water comes in. Sugar goes out.

Xylem

Carries water and dissolved mineral ions up from the roots to the leaves. Hollow tubes of dead cells, with walls strengthened by lignin.

Phloem

Carries dissolved sugars from the leaves to the rest of the plant. Tubes of living, elongated cells; cell sap moves from one cell to the next through pores in the end walls.

How water is pulled up the xylem, and how fast, is the transpiration lesson.

The fifth tissue · meristem

New cells, at the growing tips.

{{ plantFig }}

Meristem tissue is found at the growing tips of shoots and roots. It makes new cells, and these can differentiate into any type of plant cell, throughout the life of the plant.

So a plant keeps growing longer shoots and roots for as long as it lives.

Key fact

The leaf is a plant organ. Its tissues are the epidermis, palisade mesophyll, spongy mesophyll, xylem and phloem, with guard cells around the stomata. Meristem at shoot and root tips makes new cells.

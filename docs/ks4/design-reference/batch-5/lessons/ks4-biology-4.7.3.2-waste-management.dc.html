Biodiversity

The variety of all the different species of organisms in an ecosystem. Pollution kills plants and animals, which can reduce biodiversity: it falls when species are lost from the area.

Handled properly

Treating sewage, filtering gases from chimneys and recycling all cut pollution. How sewage is treated is in Potable water.

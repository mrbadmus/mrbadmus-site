Differentiation

A cell becomes specialised as it differentiates.

As a cell differentiates, it gains different sub-cellular structures that let it carry out a certain job. Then it is a specialised cell. Tap animals or plants.

Cells that can still become many types are stem cells: in Stem cells.

Key fact

As a cell differentiates it gains the sub-cellular structures it needs for its job. Most animal cells differentiate early; many plant cells can differentiate throughout life.

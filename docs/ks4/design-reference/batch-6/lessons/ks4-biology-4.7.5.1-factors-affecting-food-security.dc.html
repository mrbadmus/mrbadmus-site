Food security

Enough food, and people able to get it.

Food security is having enough food to feed a population. Producing enough is only half of it: the food also has to reach people, and they have to be able to afford it. Tap each part.

Key fact

Food security is having enough food to feed a population. Six things threaten it: birth rate, changing diets, new pests and pathogens, environmental change, the cost of agricultural inputs, and conflict.

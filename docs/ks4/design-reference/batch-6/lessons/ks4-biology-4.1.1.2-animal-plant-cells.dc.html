Required practical 1 · observe and label

What you see down a light microscope.

With a light microscope you can see the nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane, cell wall, vacuole and chloroplasts. Ribosomes are too small to see. Iodine solution stains onion cells: the nucleus shows yellow-brown. Methylene blue stains cheek cells: the nucleus shows blue. The full method, focusing and drawing rules are in the Microscopy lesson.

Equation · magnification

Convert first: both sizes must be in the same unit. 1 mm = 1000 µm, so mm × 1000 gives µm, and µm ÷ 1000 gives mm. Magnification has no unit: write it as ×400, for example. Tap the quantity you want to find.

Key fact

Animal cells have a nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane, mitochondria and ribosomes. Plant cells have these too, plus a cellulose cell wall, a permanent vacuole and often chloroplasts.

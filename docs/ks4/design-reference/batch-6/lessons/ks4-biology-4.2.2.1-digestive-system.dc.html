Follow the meal

Each organ has a job.

The digestive system is an organ system: organs work together to digest and absorb food. Enzymes break large, insoluble molecules into small, soluble ones that can be absorbed into the bloodstream. Tap each organ, in order.

Three enzymes, three products

Where each enzyme is made, and what it makes.

Carbohydrases break carbohydrates down to simple sugars. Amylase is a carbohydrase. The products of digestion are used to build new carbohydrates, lipids and proteins; some glucose is used in respiration.

Key fact

Enzymes break large, insoluble molecules into small, soluble ones: starch to sugars, protein to amino acids, lipid to glycerol and fatty acids. They are absorbed in the small intestine.

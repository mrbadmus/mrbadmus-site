Where the sugar goes

Leaves to the rest of the plant, up or down.

Phloem tissue transports dissolved sugars from the leaves to the rest of the plant, for immediate use or storage. The movement of food molecules through phloem is called translocation. The leaves are the source; anywhere the sugar is used or stored is a sink. Send the sugar somewhere.

Phloem and xylem

Two tubes, built for two jobs.

Cut open a stem and the two tissues sit side by side. Their structure fits what they carry.

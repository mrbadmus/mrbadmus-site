Health and disease

Health is physical and mental well-being.

Diseases, both communicable and non-communicable, are major causes of ill health. Communicable diseases are caused by pathogens and can spread; non-communicable diseases can’t. Other factors, including diet, stress and life situations, can have a profound effect on both physical and mental health. Different types of disease may also interact. Tap each link.

Risk factors

Linked with a higher rate, not a certainty.

Risk factors are linked to an increased rate of a disease. They can be aspects of a person’s lifestyle, or substances in the person’s body or environment. Many diseases are caused by several factors interacting. Tap each one.

These diseases have a human cost (pain, illness, early death, families affected) and a financial cost (treatment, lost work), for a person, a local community, a nation and the world. Cancer has its own lesson: Cancer.

Key fact

Health is the state of physical and mental well-being. A risk factor is linked to an increased rate of a disease; a correlation alone does not prove it causes the disease.

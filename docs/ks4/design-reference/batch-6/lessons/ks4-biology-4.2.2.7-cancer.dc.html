Risk factors

Lifestyle, and genes.

Scientists have identified lifestyle risk factors for various types of cancer. There are also genetic risk factors for some cancers. A risk factor raises the chance of a cancer; it doesn’t make it certain. Tap each.

Cancer is non-communicable, though viruses living in cells can trigger some cancers: in Health, disease and risk factors.

Key fact

Benign tumours stay in one area and don’t invade other parts of the body. Malignant tumours are cancers: they invade neighbouring tissues and spread in the blood to form secondary tumours.

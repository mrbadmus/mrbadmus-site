What does this medicine do?

Kill the pathogen, or ease the symptoms?

Antibiotics, such as penicillin, help to cure bacterial disease by killing infective bacteria inside the body. Painkillers and other medicines treat the symptoms of disease but do not kill pathogens. Pick an illness and a medicine.

Specific bacteria should be treated with specific antibiotics. It is difficult to develop drugs that kill viruses without also damaging the body’s tissues.

Antibiotic resistance

Keeping antibiotics working.

The use of antibiotics has greatly reduced deaths from infectious bacterial diseases. However, the emergence of strains resistant to antibiotics, such as MRSA, is of great concern. Developing new antibiotics is costly and slow, and is unlikely to keep up with new resistant strains. Three ways to slow resistance down: tap each.

Key fact

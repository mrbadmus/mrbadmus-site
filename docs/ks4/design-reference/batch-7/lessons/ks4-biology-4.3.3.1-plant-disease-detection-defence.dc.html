Detecting and identifying disease

Plant diseases can be detected by: stunted growth; spots on leaves; areas of decay (rot); growths; malformed stems or leaves; discolouration; the presence of pests.

Identification can be made by: reference to a gardening manual or website; taking infected plants to a laboratory to identify the pathogen; using testing kits that contain monoclonal antibodies.

Where drugs come from

From plants, moulds and chemists.

Traditionally, drugs were extracted from plants and microorganisms. Tap each one.

Most new drugs are now synthesised by chemists in the pharmaceutical industry. However, the starting point may still be a chemical extracted from a plant.

Testing a new drug

Gate by gate.

New drugs are extensively tested for toxicity (is it harmful?), efficacy (does it work?) and dose (how much?). Most candidates fail along the way. Step through the gates.

Thalidomide, a 1950s drug for morning sickness, had not been tested for its effects on unborn babies. It caused severe birth defects, and drug testing was made much stricter.

Key fact

New drugs are tested for toxicity, efficacy and dose: in cells, tissues and animals first, then in clinical trials, then peer reviewed.

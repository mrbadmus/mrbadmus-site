Aerobic respiration

In every cell, all the time.

Cellular respiration is an exothermic reaction which is continuously occurring in living cells. Aerobic respiration uses oxygen and happens in the mitochondria. Send in the reactants.

The energy transferred supplies all the energy needed for living processes. Tap each use.

Cells that need a lot of energy, such as muscle cells and sperm cells, have many mitochondria.

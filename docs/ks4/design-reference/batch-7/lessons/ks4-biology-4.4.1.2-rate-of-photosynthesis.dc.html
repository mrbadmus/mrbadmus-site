Light intensity and the rate of photosynthesis.

Equation · rate of photosynthesis

The amount is bubbles of oxygen, or a volume of oxygen in cm3. Time must be in the unit the rate asks for: seconds ÷ 60 gives minutes, so 90 s = 1.5 min. Tap the quantity you want to find.

Higher

Light intensity and distance

Light intensity is inversely proportional to the square of the distance from the lamp. Put both distances in the same unit before you square them. Work out the light intensity at each distance on its own line, then compare.

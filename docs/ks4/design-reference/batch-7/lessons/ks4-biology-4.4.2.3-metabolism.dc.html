Building up and breaking down

Small units, big molecules.

Metabolism is the sum of all the reactions in a cell or the body. The energy transferred by respiration is used for the continual, enzyme-controlled processes of metabolism that synthesise new molecules. Pick each reaction.

Sugars, amino acids, fatty acids and glycerol are the small units: carbohydrates, proteins and lipids are built from them and broken back down to them. Why plants store starch: in Uses of glucose.

Higher

Thyroxine

Thyroxine from the thyroid gland stimulates the basal metabolic rate.

Excess protein

Protein out, as urea.

The body can’t store excess protein. Excess proteins are broken down to form urea for excretion. Urea is carried in the blood plasma to the kidneys.

Triple

The kidneys

The kidneys filter urea out of the blood, and it leaves the body in the urine.
Triple Higher

In the liver

Excess amino acids are deaminated in the liver to form ammonia. Ammonia is toxic, so it is immediately converted to urea for safe excretion.

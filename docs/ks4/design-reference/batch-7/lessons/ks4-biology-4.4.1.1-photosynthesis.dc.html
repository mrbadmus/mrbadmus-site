The equation

Written out.

Learn the word equation. Light is written over the arrow: it supplies the energy, it isn’t a reactant.

carbon dioxide + water light glucose + oxygen

Photosynthesis is endothermic: energy is transferred from the environment to the chloroplasts by light. Recognise the symbols: tap each.

Key fact

Glands and hormones

Six glands.

The endocrine system is made of glands that secrete chemicals called hormones directly into the bloodstream. The blood carries each hormone to a target organ, where it produces an effect. Tap each gland to find it.

The pituitary gland in the brain is a master gland: it secretes several hormones into the blood in response to body conditions, and these act on other glands to make them release hormones.

Key fact

Glands secrete hormones directly into the blood, which carries them to a target organ. Hormonal effects are slower than nervous ones but last longer.

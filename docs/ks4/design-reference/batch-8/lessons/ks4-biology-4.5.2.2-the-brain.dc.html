How do we know? Why is it hard?

Neuroscientists have mapped regions of the brain to particular functions in three ways. Tap each.

The complexity and delicacy of the brain make investigating and treating brain disorders very difficult. Damaged neurones are not easily repaired or replaced. Taking a sample of brain tissue, or operating, risks damaging other parts of the brain.

Key fact

Cerebral cortex: consciousness, intelligence, memory and language. Cerebellum: muscle coordination. Medulla: unconscious activities such as heartbeat and breathing.

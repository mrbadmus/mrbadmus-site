Control

The thermoregulatory centre.

Body temperature is monitored and controlled by the thermoregulatory centre in the brain (the hypothalamus). Enzymes work best near 37 °C: above about 40 °C they start to denature, and below about 35 °C is hypothermia. Tap each part of the system.

The general receptor, coordination centre and effector pattern: Homeostasis.

Key fact

Too hot: blood vessels dilate and sweat is produced; both transfer energy from the skin to the environment. Too cold: vessels constrict, sweating stops, muscles shiver.

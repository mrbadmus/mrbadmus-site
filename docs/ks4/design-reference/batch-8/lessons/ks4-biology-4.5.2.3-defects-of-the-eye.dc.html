Where does it focus?

In myopia (short sight) and hyperopia (long sight), rays of light do not focus on the retina. Spectacle lenses refract the rays so that they do. Pick a defect, then try each lens.

Treatments

Ways to correct it.

Spectacles are the usual treatment. New technologies now include hard and soft contact lenses, laser surgery to change the shape of the cornea, and a replacement lens in the eye. Tap each for a benefit and a risk.

Key fact

Myopia: distant objects blurred, light focuses in front of the retina, concave lens. Hyperopia: near objects blurred, focus behind the retina, convex lens.

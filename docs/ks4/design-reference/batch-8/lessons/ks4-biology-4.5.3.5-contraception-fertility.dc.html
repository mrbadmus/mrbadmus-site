Hormones to treat infertility

A fertility drug containing FSH and LH can be given to a woman whose own level of FSH is too low; she may then become pregnant in the normal way. In vitro fertilisation (IVF) goes further. Step through it.

Key fact

Hormonal methods stop eggs maturing or being released. Barriers, spermicides, abstinence and sterilisation stop sperm meeting an egg. IUDs prevent implantation or release a hormone.

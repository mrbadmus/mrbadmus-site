Amounts of substances in equations

The big numbers in a balanced equation are a ratio of moles. Turn a mass into moles, cross the ratio, turn the moles back into a mass: three steps, each learned on its own first.

Masses in a balanced equation

Read the equation in grams.

Multiply each coefficient by the Mr and the equation reads as masses. Conservation of mass checks it: the two sides add up.

Equation · moles from mass

The same triangle as Moles. It is used twice in a reacting-mass question: forwards for the substance you are given (Step 1), backwards for the one you want (Step 3). The ratio step between them is not a triangle.

Step 2 · n(wanted) = n(given) × wanted coefficient ÷ given coefficient

Key fact

Mass to moles (÷ Mr), cross the mole ratio from the coefficients, moles to mass (× Mr). Grams throughout. Use each coefficient once, in the ratio.

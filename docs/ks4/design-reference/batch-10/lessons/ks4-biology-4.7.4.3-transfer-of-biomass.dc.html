Where the biomass goes

1000 g of grass into a rabbit.

Producers are mostly plants and algae which transfer about 1% of the incident energy from light for photosynthesis. Then only approximately 10% of the biomass from each trophic level is transferred to the level above it. Follow 1000 g of grass. The split is illustrative; it varies between organisms.

Equation · efficiency of biomass transfer

Efficiency is the biomass that reaches the next level as a fraction of the biomass at this level. Both values must be in the same unit. Tap the quantity you want to find.

The triangle gives the efficiency as a decimal (or a fraction). Turning it into a percentage is its own line:

efficiency (%) = decimal × 100

Always divide the next level by this level. An answer over 100% means you divided the bigger number by the smaller: impossible, so turn it round.

Key fact

Only about 10% of the biomass at one trophic level reaches the next. The rest leaves as faeces (egested), as carbon dioxide and water from respiration, and as urea and water in urine. Efficiency = next level ÷ this level, then × 100.

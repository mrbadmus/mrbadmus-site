Key fact

Abiotic factors are non-living: light intensity, temperature, moisture, soil pH and mineral content, wind intensity and direction, carbon dioxide for plants, oxygen for aquatic animals. Biotic factors are living: food, new predators, new pathogens, one species outcompeting another.

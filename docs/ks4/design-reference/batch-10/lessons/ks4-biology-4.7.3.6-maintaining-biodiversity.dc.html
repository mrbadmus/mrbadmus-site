Conflicting pressures

What it costs.

Protecting biodiversity competes with other needs: the cost, land for food and housing, and people’s jobs. You should be able to explain and evaluate these conflicting pressures given appropriate information.

Information · made-up data for practice

Key fact

Five programmes maintain biodiversity: breeding programmes for endangered species; protecting and regenerating rare habitats; field margins and hedgerows on single-crop farms; reducing deforestation and carbon dioxide emissions; recycling rather than landfill. Each conflicts with cost, land or jobs.

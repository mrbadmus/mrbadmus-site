The carbon dioxide

Two arrows change.

Trees take carbon dioxide in by photosynthesis. Burning felled trees releases it. Microorganisms that decay the leftover wood respire, releasing it too. Fell the patch and watch both arrows.

Key fact

Forest is cleared for cattle, rice fields and biofuel crops. Carbon dioxide rises for two reasons: less is taken in by photosynthesis, and more is released by burning and by microorganisms decaying the wood. Habitats are lost, so biodiversity falls.

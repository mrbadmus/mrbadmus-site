Numbered levels

Count along the chain.

Trophic levels can be represented by numbers, starting at level 1 with plants and algae. Further trophic levels are numbered subsequently according to how far the organism is along the food chain.

Decomposers

Feeding without a mouth.

Decomposers break down dead plant and animal matter by secreting enzymes into the environment. Small soluble food molecules then diffuse into the microorganism. Step through it.

Key fact

Level 1 is the producer; each level after is numbered by how far along the chain the organism is. An apex predator is a carnivore with no predators. Decomposers secrete enzymes into the environment and small soluble food molecules diffuse into them.

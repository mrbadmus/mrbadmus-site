Mean, mode and median

Three averages from one table.

Information · made-up data for practice

Daisies counted in ten 1 m² quadrats: 1, 2, 2, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

Mean: add them all, divide by how many. 40 ÷ 10 = 4 per quadrat. Mode: the most common value. Median: the middle value when they are in order; with ten values, halfway between the 5th and 6th.

Equation · population estimate

The count in the quadrats is a sample. To estimate the whole population, find the mean number per square metre, then multiply by the habitat area in square metres. Tap the quantity you want to find.

With a 1 m² quadrat, the mean per quadrat is already the mean per m². With any other quadrat, two extra lines of their own:

mean per m² = mean per quadrat ÷ quadrat area

and the quadrat side is often given in centimetres. Convert the side to metres before squaring it:

50 cm = 0.5 m, so area = 0.5 × 0.5 = 0.25 m²

Key fact

Random quadrats, placed by random coordinates, give the mean number per m². Population = mean per m² × habitat area. A transect shows how distribution changes with an abiotic factor along a line.

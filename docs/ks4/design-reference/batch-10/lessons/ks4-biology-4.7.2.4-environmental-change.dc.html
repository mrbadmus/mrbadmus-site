The impact of environmental change

Temperature, water and the gases in the air change, and species move, change their timing, or disappear. The skill is judging what given data show, and what they do not.

What changes, and why.

Environmental changes affect the distribution of species in an ecosystem. These changes include temperature, availability of water, and composition of atmospheric gases. The changes may be seasonal, geographic or caused by human interaction. Tap each.

Key fact

Changes in temperature, water availability and atmospheric gases, whether seasonal, geographic or human-caused, change where species live. Evaluate given data: say what it shows, what else could explain it, and what would strengthen the conclusion.

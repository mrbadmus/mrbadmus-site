Sustainable fisheries

A fish stock is a breeding population. Catch too many, or catch them too young, and there is nothing to breed next year. Two levers bring it back.

The problem

Declining stocks.

Fish stocks in the oceans are declining. Fish is an important source of protein for billions of people. It is important to maintain fish stocks at a level where breeding continues, or certain species may disappear altogether in some areas. Tap each.

Two levers

Quotas and net size.

Control of net size and the introduction of fishing quotas play important roles in conservation of fish stocks at a sustainable level. Set the two levers and watch one stock over twenty years. The numbers are made up.

Key fact

A sustainable stock is one where breeding continues. Quotas limit how many fish are taken each year, set from population estimates. A larger mesh lets young fish escape to grow and breed. Recovery takes as long as the fish take to reach breeding age.

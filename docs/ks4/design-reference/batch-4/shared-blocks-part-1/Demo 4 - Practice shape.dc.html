{{ r.what }}
  • Seven questions in every lesson, on every route. Above the floor of five, and the same seven-question shape on all 13 pages of a batch.
  • Two on each of rungs 1–3. One right answer can be a lucky guess; two shows the pupil can do it. A rung scores when both its questions do, so the score stays out of 4.
  • One on rung 4. A 6-mark answer takes 8–10 minutes to write and mark. Two would push the lesson past 45 minutes.
  • The practice set after the ladder is fixed too: five verbatim quiz questions per route, no “show more”.

Same group, similar reactions · and the trends

Down Group 1, more reactive. Down Group 7, less.

Elements in the same group have the same number of outer electrons, so they have similar chemical properties. Metals are on the left and in the middle; non-metals are on the right.

Group 1 · lose one electron

Going down, each atom has another shell. The outer electron is further from the nucleus, so it is less strongly attracted and is lost more easily. Reactivity increases down the group.

Group 7 · gain one electron

Going down, the outer shell is further from the nucleus, so an incoming electron is less strongly attracted and is gained less easily. Reactivity decreases down the group.

Group 0 · already full

The noble gases have a full outer shell: 8 electrons, or 2 for helium. They are unreactive, all the way down the group.

The transition metals, between Groups 2 and 3, have their own lesson on the Triple route.

Shells, distance from the nucleus, attraction, then lost or gained more or less easily.
Give
A short answer: the group, the period, or the electronic structure.

Key fact

Elements are arranged in order of atomic number. The period number is the number of occupied shells; the group number is the number of outer electrons. Elements in the same group have similar chemical properties.

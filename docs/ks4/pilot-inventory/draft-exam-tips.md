## Series and parallel circuits (series-parallel-circuits)

Before you calculate anything, say which arrangement you are looking at. In series use one current for every component and add the resistances; in parallel use one p.d. for every branch and add the currents. Mixing the two rules is the commonest lost mark.

## Resistors and I-V characteristics (resistors)

In a method question, name the variable resistor and say what it is for: changing the p.d. across the component. Put the ammeter in series and the voltmeter in parallel, and say you reverse the connections to get negative values.

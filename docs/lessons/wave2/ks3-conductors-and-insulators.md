**Checked numbers.** 6.0 ÷ 0.20 = 30 Ω. Rung 1: 6.0 ÷ 0.15 = 40 Ω; 6.0 × 0.15 = 0.9; 0.15 ÷ 6.0 = 0.025. Plastic against copper: 2 000 000 000 000 ÷ 0.05 = 40 million million, so "more than a million million" holds. 6 V across 2 million million Ω gives 3 millionths of a millionth of an amp. Bands from the old bench: below 100 Ω conductor, 100 to 100 000 Ω poor conductor, above that insulator. Mercury superconducts at about 4 K, which is about minus 269 °C.

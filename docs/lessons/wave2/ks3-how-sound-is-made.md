**Which line was followed.** NC P32 is followed for production and detection. Rainford 23a's loudness and pitch outcome is taught on frequency-pitch-and-loudness. Rainford 23b (the ear, hearing problems) belongs to hearing-and-auditory-range; this page only says the eardrum vibrates and the ear turns that into nerve signals.

**Gaps and mismatches.** The page says "the ear turns these vibrations into nerve signals", not "the eardrum does", because the cochlea does the converting. Sound in a medium (P31) is sound-needs-a-medium's.

**Which line was followed.** The Rainford scheme, row 22c. It names the lesson (drawing and building circuits) and lists no outcomes or NC codes, so the NC lines were matched by their words: P41 and P42 for the meters, and the Working Scientifically lines the practical needs. The old page claimed WS6 (KS3.WS.EXP.03), which is kept.

**Gaps and mismatches.** The old page never drew a circuit diagram, and never said how to draw one. Now: a drawn symbol key, drawing rules, and three drawn circuits (right, ammeter across, voltmeter in the loop). The symbols are also current-and-circuits' (lane A); they are drawn again here because 22c is about drawing circuits.

- Feedback written for every wrong option of Rungs 1 and 2 (render only). Rung 1 C feedback names the voltmeter across the lamp, since an open switch then reads zero on both.
- Rung 4 tick 4 had a dash: reworded in `ladder_ticks` (render only).

**Checked numbers.** 3.00 V ÷ 0.30 A = 10 Ω, the old bench's lamp. Voltmeter in the loop: about 1 MΩ against 10 Ω, so the current is about 3 µA and the ammeter shows 0.00 A while the voltmeter shows the whole 3.00 V.

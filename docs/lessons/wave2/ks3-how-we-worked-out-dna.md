**Checked numbers.** Miescher 1869; Avery, MacLeod and McCarty 1944; Photo 51 taken 1952 by Franklin and Gosling; shown to Watson by Wilkins in January 1953; model published April 1953, with Franklin and Gosling's paper in the same issue of Nature; Franklin died 1958; Nobel Prize 1962 to Watson, Crick and Wilkins.

**Examiner fixes.** "Light microscope". Wilkins began the X-ray work at King's (Astbury had X-rayed DNA in Leeds in 1938). Two strands: "her measurements helped show" (the width alone did not settle it). Photo 51: the cross-shaped pattern is no longer described, only "its pattern of spots". Giving credit: "without Franklin knowing" and "did not say how much it relied on her results" replaced by undisputed lines and the paper's own words.

**Left out because unsure.** Whether Franklin knew Photo 51 was shown to Watson (disputed). Pauling's model details beyond "three strands". The phrase "phosphate" (the page says "backbone").

**Removed or moved.** Removed: the model-builder bench (`build-a-model-that-fits-all-of-it`), the confrontation (`who-discovered-what`), the "Who did what" rule cards (now a reveal table) and the summary note. Reused: the drawn base-pairs figure. Moved to Dive deeper: why the shape mattered (copying); DNA was known long before 1953.

**Easy mistakes rule.** GENE-05 and NOS-03 are tagged on the Easy mistakes card. Two untagged pairs added (Photo 51 is not a photograph of a molecule; who took the X-ray images).

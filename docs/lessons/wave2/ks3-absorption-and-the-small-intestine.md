**Which line was followed.** The scheme cites NC B13 for 15b, and gives 16c the codes B11, P1, P8 (copied from the burning food row). The Visking tubing model is about absorption, so B13 is cited for it.

**Gaps and mismatches.** Rainford 16c Visking tubing had no page anywhere: now a method card, a drawn model (new figure `b3-visking`), a results table and a model table, with its limits in Dive deeper. The old page never said why food must be digested before it can be absorbed in its main text; the Visking model now shows it. The old villus figure `b3-villus-labelled` is 900 wide and scrolls on a phone; a new phone-width figure `b3-villus-lean` draws the villi on the wall and one villus magnified, with the wall one cell thick, microvilli and a capillary. The old page's fourth feature (a moist surface) is not taught: the scheme and NC do not ask for it.

**Checked numbers.** Absorbing surface about 30 m² (Mide's ruling, 31 Aug 2026: the 2014 fresh-tissue figure, no hedge). A hose 6 m long and 2.5 cm across has about 0.5 m² of inside surface, so about 60 times. Visking tubing left 15 minutes before testing.

**Left out because unsure.** The 200 to 300 m² textbook figure (ruled out by Mide). Coeliac disease is only in Rung 4, as the old page had it.

**Removed or moved.** Removed: the fold builder (`fold-builder`, fold-builder instrument), the "four features" rule panel (now a reveal table), the confrontation (`villi-and-length`), the key note, the convention note. Not shown on the lean page: figure `b3-villus-labelled`. Moved to Dive deeper: other exchange surfaces (lungs, gills).

**Checked numbers.** UK white flour has calcium, iron, thiamin (vitamin B1) and niacin added by law (Bread and Flour Regulations 1998). James Lind's trial: 1747, twelve sailors with scurvy, the pair given oranges and lemons recovered. NHS advice: a vitamin D supplement in autumn and winter.

**Checked numbers.** 340 × 0.1 = 34 m there and back, so about 17 m. Rung 1: 340 × 3.0 = 1020 m; ÷ 2 = 510 m; 340 ÷ 3.0 = 113; halving twice gives 255. Cliff: 340 × 2.0 = 680; ÷ 2 = 340 m. Sea: 1500 × 1.2 = 1800; ÷ 2 = 900 m. Rung 4: 1500 × 0.40 = 600; ÷ 2 = 300 m.

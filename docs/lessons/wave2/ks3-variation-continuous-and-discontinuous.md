**Which line was followed.** Both. The scheme's 13a row asks for what variation is and its two causes; NC B36 asks for continuous and discontinuous variation, measurement and graphs. The old page taught B36 well but barely named the causes, so the causes are now a reveal table with examples.

**Gaps and mismatches.** The scheme cites B33 (heredity) for this row. Its words match the heredity lesson, so this page only names inherited variation and points there. The old page had no bar chart for discontinuous data; one is now drawn (blood group, bars apart) beside the existing height histogram (bars touching). Measurement had no card of its own; it has one now. Rainford 13c (Percentages) is used on passing-it-on-heredity, where probabilities are written as percentages.

**Quiz notes.**
- Rung 1 (stored): key C "Number of brothers and sisters" is now "Number of petals on a flower" (blind pass: a count of a real characteristic). Key letter unchanged; option D is longer. The Two kinds card says counts such as petals are discontinuous.
- Rung 2 (stored) options rewritten to 15 words or fewer, no dash; key C stays C. Old key "Nothing on its own — shape and cause are separate questions" is now "Nothing for certain, as shape and cause are separate questions". B and D now carry reasons too, so the key is not the only option with one, and D is the longest.
- Rung 3 and Rung 4 stems reworded at render only; ten ticks shortened and their dashes removed (render only). Rung 4 no longer refers to an earlier lesson ("Sampling an ecosystem"); it asks how to make the sample fair, which the Measuring variation card teaches.
- Rung 2 stem and key kept as they are (coordinator ruling). The Shape and cause card now ends "So the shape of a graph alone cannot tell you the cause", so no card contradicts the key.
- Feedback written for every wrong option of Rungs 1 and 2.

**Checked numbers.** Height chart: 3 + 7 + 13 + 16 + 12 + 6 + 3 = 60. Blood group chart: 28 + 25 + 5 + 2 = 60, close to UK proportions (O and A most common, AB rarest). Dutch men about 20 cm taller over about 150 years.

**Left out because unsure.** Tongue rolling and earlobes as examples (their genetics is not simple). Eye colour is no longer a discontinuous example (coordinator ruling: it is several genes and grades through shades); number of petals replaces it. "Natural eye colour" stays only as an inherited example.

**Removed or moved.** Removed: the variation plotter bench (`predict-the-graph-then-plot-it`), the confrontation (`shape-is-not-cause`), the "Two questions, asked separately" rule cards and the summary note. Their content is in the reveal tables and the Shape and cause card. Moved to Dive deeper: why people have grown taller; shade leaves on one tree.

**Easy mistakes rule.** GENE-01 and GENE-02 are tagged on the Easy mistakes card. GENE-02 pair reworded after the examiner pass: the wrong line is now "If you can put a number on it, it is continuous" (a ruler measure is always continuous, so the old wrong line was not wrong). Two untagged pairs added (histogram gaps; genes or environment, never both).

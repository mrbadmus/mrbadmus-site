**Checked numbers.** Young human about 20 Hz to 20 000 Hz; at about 50, the top is about 12 000 Hz. Dog 67 to 45 000 Hz; elephant 16 to 12 000 Hz; bat 2000 to 110 000 Hz (the old page's values, all "about"). 30 000 Hz is above 20 000 (human) and 12 000 (elephant), below 45 000 (dog). The range figure's scale runs 1 Hz to 300 000 Hz, ten times per mark.

**Left out because unsure.** Cat and mouse ranges (old bench) are left out: three animals meet P33. The ossicles "make the vibrations stronger" (examiner: they increase the force on the cochlea, not the size of the movement).

**Which line was followed.** The scheme (23b) and NC P44 agree. Nothing differs.

**Gaps and mismatches.** None. The old page's seven-material triboelectric ladder is replaced by the rule (the material that holds electrons more tightly gains them) and three standard KS3 pairs.

**Quiz notes.**
- Rung 1 stem (stored): "Glass sits above wool on the list" named a list the lean page no longer has. Now: "Glass holds its electrons less tightly than wool." Options and key A unchanged (the key is tied for longest, never longest alone).
- Rung 2 (stored): all four options had dashes or ran to 19 to 30 words, and key C was the second longest. All four rewritten, 12 to 15 words, each with a "because" reason. Key C (KEY TEXT CHANGED): "No charge was created, because electrons moved from the jumper onto the balloon."
- Feedback written for every wrong option of Rungs 1 and 2.

**Checked numbers.** The drawn rod and duster each hold 4 protons. Before: 4 and 4 electrons. After: 6 and 2. The drawer refuses a panel whose electron total differs from the other panel's.

**Left out because unsure.** The exact order of a triboelectric series (published lists disagree). Only the polythene/wool, acetate/cloth and balloon/hair results are given, which are the standard KS3 results.

**Removed or moved.** Removed: the rub bench (`rub`, transfer-pair), the triboelectric ladder figure (`tribo`), the confrontation (`think-nothing-is-created`, charge-think), the summary. Moved to Dive deeper: static at home, fuel and sparks (earthing).

**Easy mistakes rule.** CHRG-01, CHRG-02, CHRG-03 and CHRG-04 are tagged. One untagged pair: a held metal rod does not stay charged.

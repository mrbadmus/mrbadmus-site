**Which line was followed.** The Rainford scheme (17b). NC P54 and C12 both match its words and are cited.

**Gaps and mismatches.** None for the scheme. The old page had no list of signs of a chemical change; the scheme asks for one, so the card Signs of a chemical change was added, each sign marked as a clue that some physical changes also show.

**Examiner fixes.** Sugar row Why cell → "It is still sugar, and sets solid as it cools" (melted sugar sets glassy, not granular). Dive deeper potato line → "Melting butter in a pan is physical. It sets again in the fridge." (cooking a potato is usually called chemical). Rung 4 tick 5 and placeholder as in Quiz notes.

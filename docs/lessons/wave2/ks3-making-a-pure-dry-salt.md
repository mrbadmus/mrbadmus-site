**Which line was followed.** The Rainford scheme (19a, 19b). Rainford 19a has no page of its own and is taught here, beside 19b. The scheme cites C27 for 19a; its words (metal oxides and acidity) match the metal oxide reaction used to make the salt, so it is kept.

**Gaps and mismatches.** Rainford 19a (Salts) has no page of its own: naming, the word equations for all three reactions and uses of salts are taught here. The old page had no uses of salts in Key knowledge (only in a stretch paragraph) and no word equations for metals or carbonates; both are added. The old fertiliser example (ammonium nitrate) is replaced with potassium nitrate, because ammonium is not a metal and the naming rule on the page says the first word is the metal. Making a salt from an acid and an alkali is taught in full on the neutralisation page (Rainford 17b); here it is one card, because Rung 4 asks for it.

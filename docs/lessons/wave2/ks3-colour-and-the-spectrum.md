**Which line was followed.** The scheme (22b, 24b). The scheme cites P30 to P32 (sound) for 22b and P29 (water waves) for 24b; P40 is the line whose words match 22b. The KS3 national curriculum has no line for the electromagnetic spectrum, so 24b follows the scheme alone.

**Gaps and mismatches.** Rainford 24b has no page of its own: it is taught here as a card and a two-column reveal table (Uses and dangers), as the unit notes allow. The lesson file's own misconception statement LIGHT-22 still says "seven colours" (register text, not edited by this lane).

**Which line was followed.** The Rainford scheme (18b). No NC line names carbonates. The scheme cites C17, whose words are about metals; the nearest NC words are C15 (a carbonate neutralises an acid). Both are cited and the scheme's content was followed.

**Gaps and mismatches.** The old page said limewater goes milky "for carbon dioxide and for nothing else". That is not quite true (sulfur dioxide also turns it milky), so the page says only that limewater turning milky is the test for carbon dioxide. The old page called the white solids "four white solids" including baking soda (a hydrogencarbonate); the word equations use plain carbonates only.

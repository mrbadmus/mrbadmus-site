**Checked numbers.** Meters loop: 3.0 V battery, one lamp, 3.0 V across it. Series: 2.0 + 4.0 = 6.0 V. Worked: 4.5 − 1.8 = 2.7 V; 4500 mV ÷ 1000 = 4.5 V, 12 − 4.5 = 7.5 V. Parallel: 6.0 V on each branch. Four 1.5 V cells in series = 6.0 V. Rung 1: 6.0 − 1.5 − 3.0 = 1.5 V. One volt = one joule per coulomb, so 12 V gives each coulomb 12 J.

**Definition (commander ruling, fix round).** Potential difference is defined as "the energy each unit of charge transfers between two points" everywhere it is defined. "You can picture it as a push" is a model only, never the definition. Start here reply A now says the number is in volts, the p.d. the bulb needs, not an amount it uses up. The worked examples use a lamp and a resistor in place of the old page's lamp and buzzer, because a buzzer has no symbol in the 21a set.

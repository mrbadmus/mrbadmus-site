**Gaps and mismatches.** None against the scheme or NC. The old page's five jobs are now four rows of a reveal table: "maintaining the gut wall" is left out, as KS3 does not need it, and the germ-free mouse's thinner gut wall is kept in Dive deeper. The old page drew nothing and needs no figure.

**Quiz notes.**
- Rung 1 (recall): key D "Break down cellulose and make vitamin K" → "Break down fibre and make vitamin K" (KEY TEXT CHANGED, examiner: human gut bacteria break down only part of the cellulose; "fibre" is the page's word). A "Absorb glucose from the gut into the blood" → "Absorb glucose and pass it into the blood", so the key is not the only two-part option (blind pass). The key is still the shortest.
- Rung 2 (apply), stem shortened ("broad-spectrum" dropped). All four options now "The X, so Y". Key B "The antibiotic cleared the competing species, leaving space and food for C. difficile" → "The antibiotic killed competing bacteria, so C. difficile had space and food" (KEY TEXT CHANGED; was the longest). A "The antibiotic made C. difficile itself stronger and harder to kill" → "The antibiotic made C. difficile much stronger, so it was much harder to kill". C "Antibiotics irritate the gut and cause diarrhoea directly, with no bacterium involved" → "The antibiotic irritated the gut, so it caused diarrhoea with no bacterium". D "The chest infection spread down to the gut and multiplied there" → "The chest infection spread down into the gut, so its bacteria multiplied there".
- Rungs 3 and 4 stems rewritten in short sentences (render only); Rung 3 no longer names "the five jobs" (the switch panel is gone). Seven ticks shortened, dashes removed.
- Feedback written for every wrong option of Rungs 1 and 2.

**Checked numbers.** Germ-free mice need about 30% more food (the old page's figure). Gut bacteria live at about 37 °C. UK newborns are offered vitamin K at birth.

**Examiner fixes (18 Nov).** Dive deeper vitamin K card: newborns have little vitamin K because little crosses from the mother and their gut has almost no bacteria yet (the old "so" made bacteria the only cause).

**Left out because unsure.** Bacterial cell and gene counts (the old page's "several million genes" and "thirty trillion" were flagged as estimates that change); the page says "trillions". The faecal transplant stretch explainer and its "nine cases out of ten" figure (old flag 23 asked whether it is wanted at KS3).

**Removed or moved.** Removed: the job switch (`job-switch`, job-switch instrument), the "both sides of the deal" rule panel (now the card Good for both sides), the confrontation (`germs-and-simplicity`), the key note, the convention note, the faecal transplant explainer. Moved to Dive deeper: the germ-free mouse; vitamin K for newborn babies (from the old job card).

**Easy mistakes rule.** DIET-17 and CELL-08 tagged. Two untagged pairs: antibiotics only kill harmful bacteria; most gut bacteria live in the stomach.

**Which line was followed.** Rainford 18a ("use temperature data") and 19c (investigating) are followed. NC C21 is qualitative, so the only calculation is temperature change = final temperature − start temperature, as the unit notes ask, with a CFIFA worked example on a drawn thermometer pair and on a drawn results table before any rung.

**Gaps and mismatches.** Rainford 19c has no page of its own: the method, apparatus, fair test and errors are taught here. Random and systematic error (ENER-07, ENER-08) come from NC WS14, not from the scheme row; they are kept because the old page and Rung 2 need them. The old page's plan critique said stirring with the thermometer is a flaw; many school methods stir with it, so the page just says "Stir" and does not rule on it.

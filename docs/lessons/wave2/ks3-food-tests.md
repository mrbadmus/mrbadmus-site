**Which line was followed.** The Rainford scheme (13b, 14a). NC has no food-test line; the scheme cites B10 for the nutrients and the tests are its own content.

**Gaps and mismatches.** The old page tested iodine by dropping it onto the food; the scheme's colours are orange-brown to blue-black, which the page now draws and states. The old page gave Benedict's only as blue to brick red; the scheme's green, yellow and orange positives are added. The old page used "lilac purple" for Biuret; the page says purple (lilac). The paper (grease spot) test for fat is not used; the old page used the ethanol emulsion test.

All four options now begin "The food…". Key C "The cream contains lipid" → "The food contains lipid" (KEY TEXT CHANGED).

The page says saliva breaks some of the starch down into sugar.
- Rungs 3 and 4 stems split into short sentences (render only). Ticks shortened, dashes removed. The sucrose tick now asks only that a blue Benedict's result does not prove there is no sugar (table sugar is in Dive deeper).
- Feedback written for every wrong option of Rungs 1 and 2, matching the current options.
- Rung 4 tick now says the tests "do not measure exact amounts".

**Examiner fixes.** Card "A test shows if, not exactly how much" rewritten in the examiner's words. Key fact gives the full Benedict's range. Dive deeper: neutralise the acid before adding Benedict's.

**Checked numbers.** Benedict's: about five minutes in a hot water bath. Potato is about 2% protein (false negative with Biuret).

**Left out because unsure.** Hazard classes for Benedict's and Biuret solutions (they depend on the concentration a school makes up). The safety note says only what is safe for every version: wear eye protection, Biuret contains sodium hydroxide, never taste anything.

**Removed or moved.** Removed: the twenty-combination test bench (`twenty-combinations`), the confrontation (`think-colour`), the "three questions" rule cards (now two cards), the summary note. Moved to Dive deeper: table sugar does not show with Benedict's; comparing amounts fairly.

**Easy mistakes rule.** DIET-04 and DIET-05 are tagged. Two untagged pairs: Benedict's needs heating; iodine is the starch test, not the sugar test.

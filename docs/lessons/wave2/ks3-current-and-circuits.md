**Which line was followed.** The scheme (21a, 22c) and the NC line (P41) agree. The scheme cites WS11 (present data) for 21a; P41 is the line whose words match the page's current content, and no NC line names circuit symbols.

**Gaps and mismatches.** The old page drew eight symbols. Rainford 21a and the unit notes want the switch closed, the wire and the junction as well, so the page now draws eleven, in two tables. The old p8-lamp-symbol figure stays in the lesson's figures (a bank question points at it) but is not placed on the lean page.

**Checked numbers.** Both ammeters 0.30 A in the drawn loop (Rung 1 uses 0.24 A, so the figure does not hand over the answer). 1 A = 1 C/s; 1 C ≈ 6.24 × 10^18 electrons, "about six million million million". Drift speed in a lamp flex: well under 1 mm/s.

**Which line was followed.** The scheme cites NC B10 (a healthy diet) for 15a. The NC line whose words match the digestive system is B13, so B13 is cited.

**Gaps and mismatches.** The old page never drew the organs where they sit in the body, so a pupil could not practise labelling them (the scheme's first outcome). A new phone-width figure `b3-gut-body` draws them in a body outline with every organ labelled. The old figure `b3-gut-labelled` drew the gut as one straightened tube, 900 wide, with numbers instead of names; it stays in the lesson's figures, unused on the lean page. Role of each organ is now one reveal table.

**Quiz notes.**
- Rung 1 (recall): unchanged. The key "The small intestine" ties with "The large intestine" for length.
- Rung 2 (apply), all four options rewritten to one frame, "X, so Y". Key B "Bile emulsifies fat into small droplets, giving lipase far more surface to work on" → "Bile breaks fat into small droplets, so lipase has more surface" (KEY TEXT CHANGED, no longer the longest). C was "Bile neutralises stomach acid, and without it lipase is destroyed": bile does help neutralise the acid, so C was not definitely wrong; now "Bile adds lipase to the gut, so without it no lipase arrives". A "Bile digests fat, so without it fat is not broken down" → "Bile digests the fat itself, so without bile no fat is broken down". D "Without a gall bladder the liver stops making bile" → "Without a gall bladder the liver stops making bile, so no fat is digested" (now the longest).
- Rungs 3 and 4: Rung 4 stem split into short sentences (render only); six ticks shortened, dashes removed.
- Feedback written for every wrong option of Rungs 1 and 2.

**Checked numbers.** Time in the stomach 2 to 4 hours; small intestine about 4 hours; large intestine about a day or more (the old page's own corrected figures, MRB-352). Small intestine about 6 to 7 metres long.

**Left out because unsure.** Exact transit times as a chart (`b3-digestion-timing` is 720 wide and scrolls on a phone); the times are given in words in Dive deeper.

**Removed or moved.** Removed: the seven-stop gut journey (`seven-stops`), the "Two kinds of breaking" rule panel (now a reveal table), the confrontation (`two-wrong-ideas`), the key note, the convention note, the "gut is outside you" stretch explainer. Not shown on the lean page: figures `b3-gut-labelled` and `b3-digestion-timing`. Moved to Dive deeper: how long food stays in each part; egestion and excretion.

**Easy mistakes rule.** DIET-11 and DIET-12 tagged. DIET-11's wrong line now reads "Digestion is only food being squashed into smaller pieces" (squashing is mechanical digestion, so the old line was not definitely wrong). Two untagged pairs: food passes through the liver and pancreas; bile is an enzyme.

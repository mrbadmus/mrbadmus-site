**Checked numbers.** Water: 2 H2 + O2 = 4 H, 2 O; 2 H2O = 4 H, 2 O. Methane: CH4 + 2 O2 = 1 C, 4 H, 4 O (9 atoms); CO2 + 2 H2O = 1 C, 4 H, 4 O (9 atoms).

**Left out because unsure.** The old bench "built" hydrogen peroxide from hydrogen and oxygen. Hydrogen and oxygen do not normally react to give hydrogen peroxide, so the page only says both substances are made of the same two kinds of atom (Dive deeper).

**Checked numbers.** Rung 1: 1100 + 1750 + 320 + 2400 + 2 × 1050 = 7670 kJ; 9500 − 7670 = 1830, about 1800 short. One bar only: 6620; 9500 − 6620 = 2880, about 2900 short. Rung 2: 1050 ÷ 5800 × 100 = 18.1%. Worked example: 4 × 780 = 3120; 3120 + 2400 + 450 + 1050 = 7020 kJ; 9500 − 7020 = 2480 kJ. 1050 ÷ 9500 × 100 = 11.05%, about 11%. Daily needs (UK estimates, rounded): 4-year-old about 5800 kJ; 13-year-old about 9500 kJ; desk worker about 9000 kJ; bricklayer about 13 500 kJ; rower in training about 25 000 kJ. Pregnancy: about 800 kJ more a day in the last three months (SACN: 200 kcal). Energy in 1 g: fat about 37 kJ, carbohydrate and protein about 17 kJ, wood about 16 kJ, coal about 30 kJ, petrol about 46 kJ, natural gas about 55 kJ. Dive deeper: 20 × 4.2 × 25 = 2100 J = 2.1 kJ.

**Checked numbers.** 680 ÷ 340 = 2.0 s; 340 ÷ 680 = 0.50; 680 × 340 = 231 200 (about 231 000). Rail: 1000 ÷ 5000 = 0.2 s; 1000 ÷ 340 = 2.94, about 2.9 s. Pipe: 2.4 km = 2400 m; 2400 ÷ 0.48 = 5000 m/s. Rung 4: 1700 ÷ 5000 = 0.34 s; 1700 ÷ 340 = 5.0 s; gap about 4.7 s. Steel is about 15 times air (5000 ÷ 340 = 14.7). Speed in air rises by about 0.6 m/s per °C.

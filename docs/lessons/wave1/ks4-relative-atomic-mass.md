**Calculations (recomputed).** Chlorine: [(75 × 35) + (25 × 37)] ÷ 100 = (2625 + 925) ÷ 100 = 3550 ÷ 100 = 35.5; figure: (35 + 35 + 35 + 37) ÷ 4 = 142 ÷ 4 = 35.5. Boron: [(20 × 10) + (80 × 11)] ÷ 100 = 1080 ÷ 100 = 10.8; unbracketed 200 + 880 ÷ 100 = 200 + 8.8 = 208.8. Copper: 100 − 69 = 31%; [(69 × 63) + (31 × 65)] ÷ 100 = (4347 + 2015) ÷ 100 = 63.62 = 63.6. Gallium: (4140 + 2840) ÷ 100 = 69.8. Bromine 1 : 1: (3950 + 4050) ÷ 100 = 80. Lithium: 100 − 7.5 = 92.5%; (45 + 647.5) ÷ 100 = 6.925 = 6.9. Rubidium (Rung 2): (6120 + 2436) ÷ 100 = 85.56 = 85.6. Thallium (Rung 2): 3 : 7 = 30% and 70%; (6090 + 14 350) ÷ 100 = 204.4. Magnesium (Rung 4): (1896 + 250 + 286) ÷ 100 = 2432 ÷ 100 = 24.3. Practice Q2: (3780 + 2600) ÷ 100 = 63.8. Silicon (Dive deeper): [(92 × 28) + (5 × 29) + (3 × 30)] ÷ 100 = (2576 + 145 + 90) ÷ 100 = 28.11 = 28.1. Chlorine neutrons: 35 − 17 = 18, 37 − 17 = 20.

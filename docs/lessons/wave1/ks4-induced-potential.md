Rainford outcome "explain why relative movement of a wire through a magnetic field is required" is covered by the reveal table and Dive deeper: Across, not along. Rainford's key word "electromotive force" is not AQA GCSE wording and is not used.

**Gaps and mismatches.** The old page never used AQA's sentence about the induced current's field opposing the original change; it gave "Lenz's Law" instead. It had no worked context for the direction of the opposing field (which pole the coil makes). It did not say a p.d. is induced even with no complete circuit.

**Removed or moved.** Removed: Faraday's law, Lenz's law and "magnetic flux" (not AQA GCSE wording; replaced by the spec's own sentences). Removed: Fleming's right-hand rule (not on the AQA spec). Removed: coil cross-sectional area as a factor (true, but not one of the factors AQA mark schemes ask for; kept to speed, magnet strength and turns). Moved to uses-generator-effect: the whole "Simple generators" section (alternator, slip rings, why a.c. is produced) and the split-ring commutator. Removed: back-emf in motors (not on the spec). Removed: the matching activity. Quiz: Q1 "A bar magnet is pushed into a coil of wire. What induces a current, and how can the induced current be increased?": **question wording kept exactly, so its quiz ID is unchanged**; marked "fixed" because the options and feedback were rewritten. Its options used "changing magnetic flux" and the right option was visibly the longest; they now use AQA wording ("the changing magnetic field"). Q2 "Why does a simple AC generator produce alternating current rather than direct current?" removed, ID dropped: it rested on "emf" and "cuts field lines … sinusoidally", and the alternator is taught in Uses of the generator effect, not here. Replaced by "Which of these induces a potential difference across the ends of a wire?" (moving the wire across a magnetic field), which tests this lesson's spec sentence. Q3 (reversing the induced current): new; its credited option reads "Pushing the other pole in first", because "turning the magnet round" only reverses the current if the other pole now goes in. Q4 (magnet held still, ammeter reads zero) and Q5 (which pole the coil makes): new. Rung 2 Q2 now says "two changes that would each make the current bigger", so the mixed distractor ("a weaker magnet and move it faster") is definitely wrong.

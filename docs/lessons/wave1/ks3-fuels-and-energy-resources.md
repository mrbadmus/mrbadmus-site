**Checked facts.** Coal from swamp plants; oil and gas from plankton; buried with little oxygen; heat and pressure over millions of years. Coal uses include power stations and steelmaking (coke). Burning coal and oil can release sulfur dioxide (acid rain). Tidal and geothermal energy do not come from the Sun.

**Left out because unsure.** Any numbers for carbon dioxide per kWh (the table says "a lot", "less than coal", "almost none", "none"). Land area per resource (old grid axis).

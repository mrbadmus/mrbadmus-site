**Gaps and mismatches.** Rainford 10a (work done) has no site lesson of its own. It is taught here, because this lesson already used force × distance. No new page was made. On the site, the "Before this lesson" link is Insulation. Rainford teaches insulation later, in Phys 6. The link is lesson data and was not changed.

**Checked numbers.** 80 N × 2.5 m = 200 J; 80 × 250 = 20 000 (100 times too big). Lever: 100 N × 0.6 m = 60 J = 600 N × 0.1 m; arms 1.2 m and 0.2 m (ratio 6). Rung 1: 150 N × 0.40 m = 60 J; distractors 150 × 40 = 6000, 150 ÷ 0.40 = 375, 150 + 40 = 190. Rung 2: 900 N × h = 300 N × 3h.

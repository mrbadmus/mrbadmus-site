**Gaps and mismatches.** The SOW teaches animal cells (1b) and plant cells (2a) as two lessons; the site has one page for both. SOW 1b's first outcome ("all living things are made from cells") is on Life processes.

**Question audit fixes (docs/b2c/QUESTION-AUDIT.md).**

- `ks3-b1-animal-and-plant-cells-explain` (why mitochondria and membrane cannot be seen): now Card: What a school microscope can show (relative sizes, membrane a few molecules thick and pressed to the wall, a light microscope's detail limit).

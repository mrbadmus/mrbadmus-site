**Gaps and mismatches.**
- The old page taught the whole Triple decomposition topic, required practical and all, on CF and CH. Combined pupils were taught (and quizzed on) content that is not on 8464. Now gated to TF/TH.
- The old page called the practical "RP7" and described bread mould or respirometers. AQA's Triple practical is **Required practical activity 10**, on fresh milk and pH change; Combined has no decay practical (its Required practical activity 7 is sampling). Fixed.
- The old page never stated "returning ... mineral ions to the soil" in the spec's words, never mentioned compost as a natural fertiliser, and had no rate calculation or graph work. All now covered on the right routes.
- The spec code was "4.7.3". The right codes are 4.7.2.2 (all routes) and 4.7.2.3 (TF, TH).

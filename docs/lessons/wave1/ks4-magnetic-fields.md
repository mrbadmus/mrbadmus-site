**Gaps and mismatches.** Every known fault in the brief was in the old page and is fixed:
- The old page called compass plotting "RP21". It is not an AQA required practical; there is none in this unit. The record now treats it as the "describe how to" skill it is, and does not mention a required practical.
- The old page left out the four magnetic materials (iron, steel, cobalt and nickel). Now a card, an Easy mistake, the Key fact, Rung 1 and a quiz question.
- The old page left out "the force between a magnet and a magnetic material is always one of attraction". Now a card line, an Easy mistake, Rung 2 and a quiz question.
- The old page left out an induced magnet losing most or all of its magnetism quickly. That is 6.7.1.1, now taught in Poles of a magnet.
- The old page never linked the compass to evidence that the Earth's core must be magnetic. Now a card with the reasoning step by step, and a chain rung.
- The old page also never gave AQA's definition of field direction ("the force that would act on another north pole placed at that point"). Now a card and Rung 1.

**Removed or moved.**
- Removed: "RP21" (see above).
- Removed: iron filings, neutral points, the field between attracting and repelling magnets, and horseshoe magnets. None is on 6.7.1.2 or 4.7.1.2, and none of them helps a pupil with the four skills the spec asks for, so they are not in Dive deeper either.
- Removed: "inside the magnet the arrows point from south to north" and "field lines form closed loops". True, but not on the spec, and pupils mix them up with the N-to-S rule they must write.
- Kept as a drawing rule, not a spec line: "Field lines never cross".
- Moved to Dive deeper: why the north pole is called north seeking (it points to geographic north, so the Earth's magnetic pole there is a south pole).
- Removed: the matching activity on field lines. Its content is in the Drawing card and Easy mistakes.
- Quiz 1 (iron filings clustering near the poles): rewritten, new ID. Iron filings are off-spec for every route, and the credited answer was visibly the longest. Replaced with "Where is the magnetic field of a bar magnet strongest?"
- Quiz 2 ("Which direction do magnetic field lines point outside a bar magnet?"): **question wording kept exactly, so its quiz ID is unchanged**; marked "fixed" because the options and feedback were rewritten. Why: the correct option was visibly the longest, every option had a dash tail, and the feedback used "closed loops" (removed above as off-spec) and "the direction a free north pole would move" (not AQA wording).
- Quiz 3, 4 and 5: new, identical on every route.
- Rung 2 Q2 (steel paper clip near each pole): the credited reason is now "because it becomes an induced magnet", not "because steel is a magnetic material", which an examiner would not credit as the explanation.
- New ladder written (2·2·2·1). No chain has an alternative order.

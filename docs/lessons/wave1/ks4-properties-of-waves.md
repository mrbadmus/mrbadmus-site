# properties-of-waves

Sources: AQA 8464 6.6.1.2 + RP 20 (CF CH) / 8463 4.6.1.2 + RP 8 (TF TH); the shared text is word for word the same, 8463 adds one (Physics only) statement; read from spec-8464.txt and spec-8463.txt (the 2016 specification, first examined in 2018). Rainford SOW 'Properties of waves' (wavelength, frequency, period, amplitude; wave speed from f and λ; period from f) and 'Investigating waves RP' (speed of a wave in a solid and in water; effect of changing frequency on wavelength; mean, accuracy, resolution).
Routes: CF CH TF TH.

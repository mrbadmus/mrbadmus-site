**Gaps and mismatches.** The split-ring commutator is not a spec sentence in 8464 or 8463. It is taught (one card, one Easy-mistakes line, no quiz question) because it is the standard answer to why a d.c. motor keeps turning the same way and Rainford's SOW names it as a key word. No rung or quiz question tests it, because it is not on 8464 and the quiz is served on CH. The old page had no gap against the spec sentence.

**Removed or moved.** Old-format page rebuilt. New ladder written (2·2·2·1). Quiz: question 1 ("Why does a DC motor need a split-ring commutator?") removed on CH and TH, so its ID is dropped. The split-ring commutator is not in 8464 6.7.2.3, so a question on it is off-spec on CH, and the quiz is the same on both routes. Replaced by a new on-spec question, "Which change would make an electric motor turn faster?" (increase the current), which does not repeat Q3 (turn the other way), Q4 (stronger magnet) or any rung. Question 2 (regenerative braking) fixed into an on-spec question: it tested the generator effect, which is not on 8464 at all, used energy forms ("kinetic energy → electrical energy") that AQA no longer uses, and its own feedback admitted option 4 was partly right. Questions 3, 4 and 5 new. All five are identical on CH and TH. Removed: the applications list (consumer electronics, transport, industry, medical), the efficiency comparison with petrol engines, regenerative braking, "torque" (the word is not in this spec section), the "add an iron core" and "armature" detail (moved, simplified, to Dive deeper: Real motors). The matching activity is not carried over.

**Misconceptions taught against (Easy mistakes).** More protons means the outer electron is held more tightly; the gas from water is oxygen; Group 1 gets less reactive going down.

**Gaps and mismatches.** None.

**Equations (every one checked by counting atoms).** 2M + 2H₂O → 2MOH + H₂: M 2 = 2, H 4 = 2 + 2, O 2 = 2. 4M + O₂ → 2M₂O: M 4 = 4, O 2 = 2. 2M + Cl₂ → 2MCl: M 2 = 2, Cl 2 = 2 (M = Li, Na, K). Word equations for sodium with water, oxygen and chlorine.

**Gaps and mismatches.** The old page never taught the newtonmeter or turning; both are now in. Weight as a force (Rainford 9b) is on `mass-vs-weight`, which is held (see NOTES).

**Quiz notes.**
- Keys checked: Rung 1 A (the head and ball push each other equally); Rung 2 B (a force acts across a gap).
- Rung 3 and Rung 4 stems split into short sentences (render only). Four ticks shortened to 15 words or fewer.
- Feedback rewritten for every wrong option of Rungs 1 and 2 (the stored Rung 1 line had a semicolon sentence of 22 words).
- Rung 4 (rocket) is answered by the pairs card's swimmer: push the water or gas backwards, and it pushes you forwards.

**Checked numbers.** Hard kick about 1000 N (0.43 kg to 25 m/s in about 0.01 s; the old page said 300 N). Apple about 1 N (100 g × 10 N/kg). Shoe 3 N on a 0 to 10 N scale. Earth and Moon about 2 × 10²⁰ N, written as "200 billion billion N" as the old page did.

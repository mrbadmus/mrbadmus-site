**Gaps and mismatches.** The old page gave differences only; the SOW asks for similarities too, now a card. Rung 4 (bacteria killed by oxygen, which came first) was not taught on the old page; now a Key knowledge card.

**Science changed.** The old page said aerobic gets "about twenty times more" energy per glucose. The ratio depends on the count used (about 15 to 19 times), and AQA only asks for "much more", so the page and the Rung 3 tick now say "much more". "Anaerobic respiration must be the older process" became "probably".

**Quiz notes.** No keyed answer changed. Rungs 3 and 4 stems and eight ticks rewritten to sentences of 15 words or fewer (render only).

**Removed or moved.** Removed: the five-situations bench, the self-check panel, the two long "Think again" essays, Going further. Moved to Dive deeper: why anaerobic is faster, cells with few mitochondria.

**Review fixes (12 Nov).** Blind answer check agreed with every key. Reveal table gained a Reactants row and the key fact names the products (NC B29). "Yeast and many bacteria use it all the time" contradicted the fermentation page; now "Yeast uses it whenever oxygen is short".

**Unsure (left out until checked).**
- "Angle of incidence = angle of reflection" is not spec wording. Kept: constructing a reflection ray diagram (the spec's own skill) needs it, and AQA mark schemes credit it.
- TH-only Dive deeper example, reworded by the examiner (14 Nov 2026) so it is not overstated: "A brick wall absorbs or reflects visible light, so you cannot see through it. Radio waves are mostly transmitted through the wall, with some absorbed, so a radio still works indoors." It illustrates the (HT only) 4.6.2.2 line, so it is TH only.
- "A dark road warms up in sunshine because it absorbs the Sun's radiation": everyday example of absorption, not spec wording.

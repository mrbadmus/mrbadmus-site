**Misconceptions taught against (Easy mistakes).** Mg²⁺ has 12 electrons because magnesium is element 12; the mass number is the number of neutrons; neutrons cancel the protons' charge; an atom becomes an ion by gaining or losing protons.

**Calculations (recomputed).** 0.1 nm = 0.1 × 10⁻⁹ m = 1 × 10⁻¹⁰ m. 1 × 10⁻¹⁰ m ÷ 10 000 = 1 × 10⁻¹⁴ m. Scale: 100 m ÷ 10 000 = 0.01 m = 1 cm. ²³₁₁Na: 23 − 11 = 12 neutrons. ²⁷₁₃Al³⁺: 27 − 13 = 14 neutrons, 13 − 3 = 10 electrons. ³⁵₁₇Cl⁻: 35 − 17 = 18 neutrons, 17 + 1 = 18 electrons. ⁵⁶₂₆Fe: 56 − 26 = 30. Lithium-7 (figure): 3 protons, 7 − 3 = 4 neutrons, 3 electrons as 2,1.

**Unsure (left out until checked).**
- "White light is a mixture of all the colours": not spec wording, needed for "all wavelengths reflected equally appears white".
- The coloured-light table (red, green, white and black objects in white, red and green light) and filter table use the idealised GCSE model: a coloured light or filter is one colour only. Real dyes and filters pass bands, so real results can differ.
- Colour names: only red and violet are named as the spectrum's ends; the full order is left to types-of-em-waves.

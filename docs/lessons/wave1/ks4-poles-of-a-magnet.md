**Gaps and mismatches.** The old page never said a permanent magnet "produces its own magnetic field" in AQA's words, and never said "induced magnetism always causes a force of attraction" as a rule (it gave the opposite-pole reason only). Both are now cards. The SOW puts poles and fields in one lesson ("Magnetic fields"); the site keeps them as two lessons, each covering its own spec point fully.

**Removed or moved.**
- Removed: magnetic domains, the Curie temperature, magnetising by stroking or with a d.c. solenoid, demagnetising by heating, hammering or a.c., and "hard" and "soft" magnetic materials. None is in 6.7.1.1 or 4.7.1.1 on any route.
- Removed: "ferromagnetic" and "alnico". Not AQA words.
- Moved to Dive deeper: why an induced magnet always attracts (the near end becomes the opposite pole). True and it helps, but the spec only asks for the fact.
- Removed: the matching activity (permanent / induced / both attract / demagnetising). Its content is now the reveal table; the demagnetising row was off-spec.
- Quiz 1 ("A steel paper clip is brought near a bar magnet. Why is the paper clip attracted?"): **question wording kept exactly, so its quiz ID is unchanged**; marked "fixed" because the options and feedback were rewritten. Why: the correct option was visibly the longest, every option carried a dash tail, and "The magnetic field passes through the paper clip, pulling it forward" was arguable rather than definitely wrong. Feedback used "ferromagnetic".
- Quiz 2 ("A fridge magnet and a compass are both magnets. What is the key difference…"): rewritten, new ID. The question asked for a difference and the credited answer said there is none; it also said a compass must "always point north reliably", which is loose (it points along the Earth's field), and one distractor contradicted itself. Replaced with "Which statement about permanent and induced magnets is correct?"
- Quiz 3 (two north poles facing): new, identical on every route.
- Quiz 4 ("Why is the force between two magnets called a non-contact force?"): new. Examiner fix: the distractor "It only acts on magnets that touch" said the same as "The magnets must touch to push", so it is now "It is caused by friction".
- Quiz 5: new, "Which of these is an induced magnet?" (an iron nail near a magnet). It replaces a draft question that repeated Start here and Rung 3 chain 2 (a nail picked up, then pulled off).
- Rung 2 Q2: the credited option is now "It is a magnetic material, not a permanent magnet". The draft's "It is an induced magnet, because neither end is repelled" was loose: the bar is an unmagnetised magnetic material that becomes an induced magnet near the magnet.
- New ladder written (2·2·2·1). No chain has an alternative order.

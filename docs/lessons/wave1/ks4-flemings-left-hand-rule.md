**Gaps and mismatches.** The old page had no gap against the spec statements, but it had two faults. (1) Its first quiz question was wrong: current to the right in a downward field gives a force INTO the page by Fleming's left-hand rule, and the page keyed "out of the page". (2) The old Key note listed "loudspeakers" as an application on the Combined page; loudspeakers are Physics only (4.7.2.4). The SOW asks pupils to "describe how the force can be increased": covered by the factors card, which is the spec's own list.

**Removed or moved.** Old-format page rebuilt. New ladder written (2·2·2·1). Quiz: question 1 fixed (wrong answer key, and it described a diagram in words), new ID. Question 2 ("How can the direction of force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field be reversed?"): **question wording kept exactly, so its quiz ID is unchanged**; marked "fixed" because the options and feedback were rewritten (the credited option was visibly the longest, with dash tails and capitals). Questions 3, 4 and 5 new, identical on CH and TH. No quiz item asks a pupil to find a direction with the rule, because that needs a diagram and a quiz item cannot show one; the new Key knowledge card "Using the rule" (fig:flhr-question with a reveal table) teaches it instead. Removed: Fleming's right-hand rule and the generator effect (not on 8464 at all, and not required by 8463), the garbled "FBI" memory line, the applications list (meters, maglev trains, particle accelerators: not on spec), the "stronger field on one side" catapult-field explanation (replaced by one AQA-safe sentence: the current's field and the magnet's field interact). Moved to Dive deeper: a conductor parallel to the field feels no force, where the motor effect is used (motors only, so the Combined page carries nothing Physics-only), command words. The matching activity is not carried over (the lean page has no matching block).

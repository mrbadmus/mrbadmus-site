**Gaps and mismatches.** SOW 5a is one lesson; the site splits it across this page (define atom), Elements (the periodic table) and Chemical symbols (symbols both ways). Dalton's model is National Curriculum (C3) with no SOW outcome of its own. The old page never listed Dalton's claims as a plain list, never said which two were wrong, and never taught what the model cannot explain, though Rungs 2 to 4 need all three. All three are now Key knowledge cards.

**Quiz notes.**

- Rung 2 key (B) says "the parts that are wrong are outside chemistry". That is loose: atoms of one element with different masses matter to relative atomic mass at GCSE. The page does not repeat the phrase; it says no chemical reaction changes one kind of atom into another.

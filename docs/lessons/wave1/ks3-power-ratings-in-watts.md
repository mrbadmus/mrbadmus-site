**Gaps and mismatches.** Rainford writes P = W/t with W as work done; the site lesson uses energy transferred. The page says both. Rainford 10a Work done (W = Fd) has no lesson in this site unit; see P1 simple-machines.

**Quiz notes.**
- Rung 2 key checked: kettle 2000 W × 180 s = 360 kJ; router 15 W × 28 800 s = 432 kJ, so the router.
- Rung 3 stem now gives the daily times, which the old bench supplied (render only). Rung 4 stem split into short sentences.
- Three ticks rewritten: one dash, one over 15 words, and one that said a lower rating saves energy if it "does less of the job" (wrong for the LED example it gave). It now says "if it does the same job, like an LED".
- Feedback written for every wrong option of Rungs 1 and 2.

**Checked numbers.** 9000 J ÷ 30 s = 300 W. 540 000 J ÷ 180 s = 3000 W. Typical ratings: shower 8500 W, kettle 2000 W, microwave 1200 W, router 15 W, LED lamp 9 W (example values, matching the unit's questions). 1 horsepower is about 746 W ("about 750 W").

**Left out because unsure.** The old page's claim that Watt's horsepower figure was "deliberately generous". Any standby cost per year.

**Checked numbers.** Fat 37 kJ/g, carbohydrate and protein 17 kJ/g (UK labelling values). Apple about 220 kJ/100 g, cheddar about 1700 kJ/100 g, crisps 958 kJ per 50 g bag (about 1900 kJ/100 g). Bread about 10 kJ/g × 40 g = 400 kJ.

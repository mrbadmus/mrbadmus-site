Unit C7 · lesson 4 of 4

Energy changes in reactions

Every reaction moves energy. Some push it out into the room and some pull it in, and a thermometer in the beaker is how you tell which. This unit is about which way the energy goes, why nothing is ever created or destroyed doing it, and how much a leaking beaker costs you when you try to measure it.

Unit C9 · lessons 1 to 2 of 4

Metals and materials

Some metals sit in the ground as the metal itself and some have to be torn out of a rock with electricity, and the difference is one order that was worked out by putting metals into liquids and watching. This unit is about that order, what it predicts, what it costs — and then about the materials that are not metals at all.

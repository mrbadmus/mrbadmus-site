Unit C5 · lessons 4 to 5 of 5

Types of reaction

Most of the reactions you will ever meet are one of a small number of kinds. This unit is about four of them — what each one does, what it needs, and how to tell which one you are looking at when nobody says.

Unit C6 · lessons 1 to 2 of 7

Acids and alkalis

An acid and an alkali are not things you can see, weigh or smell. They are things a substance does — to a dye, to a metal, to each other — and this unit is about reading those behaviours, predicting them, and using them on purpose.

Unit C6 · lesson 7 of 7

Acids and alkalis

An acid and an alkali are not things you can see, weigh or smell. They are things a substance does — to a dye, to a metal, to each other — and this unit is about reading those behaviours, predicting them, and using them on purpose.

Unit C8 · lessons 1 to 3 of 7

The periodic table

There are about ninety elements that occur on Earth, and for most of the nineteenth century nobody could say why some of them behaved alike. This unit is about the arrangement that answered it — what a column means, what a row means, and how a table can let you describe an element nobody has found yet.

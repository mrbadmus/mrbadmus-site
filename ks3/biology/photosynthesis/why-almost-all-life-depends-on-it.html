Photosynthesis · System

Why almost all life depends on it

Start here

A mushroom is not green. It grows on rotting wood, often in the dark.

If you had to guess, where does a mushroom's food come from?

Key words

  • An organism that makes its own food by photosynthesis.

  • An organism that gets its food by eating other organisms.

  • Simple organisms that photosynthesise. Many live in water.

  • A fungus or bacterium that feeds on dead material and breaks it down.

Key knowledge

A producer makes its own food by photosynthesis.

Plants and algae are producers.

Animals cannot make their own food. They are consumers.

Consumers get their food by eating other organisms.

The arrows show the direction the energy moves.

Chlorophyll in the grass absorbs light. Photosynthesis stores its energy in glucose.

The cow uses much of this energy in respiration.

Only a small part is stored in its body and passed on.

Tiny algae called phytoplankton float near the surface of the sea.

They photosynthesise, just like plants.

About half of the world's photosynthesis happens in the sea.

So most sea food chains start with algae.

A mushroom is a fungus. It has no chlorophyll, so it cannot photosynthesise.

It feeds on dead plant material.

That material was built by photosynthesis, so the mushroom depends on it too.

Fungi and bacteria that feed on dead material are decomposers.

Coal formed from plants that died hundreds of millions of years ago.

They were buried before they could rot.

The carbon in coal came from carbon dioxide in the air back then.

The energy in coal was captured from sunlight by those plants.

Burning coal puts that carbon dioxide back into the air.

  • Wrong: Plants make oxygen for animals to breathe.

    Right: Oxygen is a waste product. Plants and animals both use it in respiration.

  • Wrong: Only plants photosynthesise.

    Right: Algae do too. About half of all photosynthesis happens in the sea.

  • Wrong: A producer is at the top of a food chain.

    Right: A producer is always at the start of a food chain.

Key fact

Almost all life depends on photosynthesis. Plants and algae use sunlight to build glucose, an energy store for food chains. Photosynthesis also adds oxygen to the air and removes carbon dioxide.

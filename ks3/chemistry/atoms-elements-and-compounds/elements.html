Elements

Key words

  • A substance made of only one kind of atom.

  • The table that lists every known element, with its symbol.

  • Two or more elements chemically joined together.

  • Two or more substances mixed together, not chemically joined.

Key knowledge

An element is a substance made of only one kind of atom.

Copper is only copper atoms. Sulfur is only sulfur atoms.

An element cannot be broken down into anything simpler.

There are over 100 known elements.

In some elements, like copper, the atoms are joined in one giant structure.

In others, the atoms join in small groups called molecules.

Each oxygen molecule is two oxygen atoms joined together.

Oxygen is still an element. It has only one kind of atom.

Every known element is listed in the periodic table.

Each element has its own name and symbol.

Most elements are metals, but not all.

Sulfur, oxygen and carbon are non-metals.

Water, air and brass are not in the periodic table. None of them is an element.

Looks cannot tell you. Different substances can look alike.

Instead, try to break the substance down.

Heat it on its own, or pass electricity through it.

If anything simpler comes out, it is not an element.

Electricity splits water into hydrogen and oxygen. So water is not an element.

Some substances were once thought to be elements.

Heating and acids could not split them.

Then chemists used electricity, and these substances split up.

So they were never elements. The list was wrong.

The early chemists were not careless. They listed what their methods could show.

The list of elements depends on the methods we have.

  • Wrong: Brass is a metal, so it is an element.

    Right: Brass is copper and zinc mixed. Being a metal does not make something an element.

  • Wrong: Anything pure is an element.

    Right: Pure means only one substance. Pure water is a compound.

  • Wrong: Sodium reacting with water means it is breaking down.

    Right: The sodium joins with the water. The hydrogen given off comes from the water.

Key fact

An element is a substance made of only one kind of atom. It cannot be broken down into anything simpler. Every known element is listed in the periodic table.

Conservation of energy

Start here

A pendulum swings a little lower each time. After a few hundred swings, it stops.

If you had to guess, is anything in the room now a tiny bit warmer?

Yes. The air and the pivot are a tiny bit warmer. The swing's energy went there.

Key words

  • Energy cannot be created or destroyed. The total stays the same.

  • A group of objects that no energy can enter or leave.

  • Spread out into the surroundings, mostly by heating, so it is hard to use.

  • A force that acts against movement between two surfaces. It heats both surfaces.

Key knowledge

Energy cannot be created or destroyed.

It can only be transferred from one store to another.

So in a closed system, the total energy never changes.

Only the way it is shared between the stores changes.

It starts at the top with 120 J in its gravitational potential store.

At the bottom, most of it is in the kinetic store.

Each swing, air resistance and friction at the pivot heat the surroundings.

When it stops, all 120 J is in thermal stores of the surroundings.

The total is 120 J all the way through.

A car that has stopped has an empty kinetic store.

Its fuel tank may still be full.

Its brakes are now hot, so their thermal store has filled.

The energy has moved. None of it has been destroyed.

Every machine obeys conservation of energy exactly.

An efficient machine wastes less of the energy put in.

The wasted energy still exists, in thermal stores of the surroundings.

Some people claim a machine can run forever with no energy supply.

Friction and air resistance always dissipate some energy by heating.

So a machine with no energy supply slows down and stops.

A machine that lights a lamp must be emptying some energy store.

If no store can keep supplying it, the claim is false.

Magnets return to the same positions each turn.

So over each turn, the magnets supply no energy.

  • Wrong: A stopped object has run out of energy.

    Right: Its kinetic store is empty. The energy is now in other stores.

  • Wrong: Friction destroys energy.

    Right: Friction transfers energy to thermal stores.

  • Wrong: Only efficient machines conserve energy.

    Right: Every machine conserves energy. Efficient ones waste less of it.

Key fact

Energy cannot be created or destroyed. In a closed system, the total energy stays the same. When friction or air resistance slows something, energy is dissipated to the surroundings by heating.

## 2. What MrBadmus is

MrBadmus is an online science learning platform for students aged roughly 11 to 16, covering Key Stage 3 and GCSE science. It sets weekly work automatically, provides lessons, practice questions, unit checks and exam-style questions, and gives parents a dashboard to see how their child is getting on and to message them.

Some parts of the service use artificial intelligence: instant marking of extended-answer questions is produced by an AI model working from published mark schemes, and the study tutor a child can ask questions of is AI-driven. Answers a child chooses to "send to Mr Badmus" are marked personally by a qualified teacher. We say which is which on every screen where it matters.

MrBadmus supports a child's learning. It is not a school, does not award qualifications, and does not guarantee any grade or outcome.

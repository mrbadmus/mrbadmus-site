Unit B4 · lesson 5 of 5

Breathing and gas exchange

You breathe about twenty thousand times a day without deciding to. This unit is about what the air is for, how it gets in and out when your lungs contain no muscle at all, and why the same problem has the same solution in a lung and in a leaf.

Unit B5 · lessons 1 to 3 of 8

Reproduction

Every living thing you have ever seen came from another living thing. This unit is about how — in humans, and in the flowering plants that solved the same problem without being able to move.

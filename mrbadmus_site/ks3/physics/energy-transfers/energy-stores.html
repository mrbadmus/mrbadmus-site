Energy stores

Start here

A ball rolls across a carpet, slows down and stops.

If you had to guess, has its energy been used up, or gone somewhere else?

It went somewhere else. The ball and the carpet warmed up very slightly.

Key words

  • A way energy is held, such as in a moving object or a fuel.

  • Energy moving from one store to another.

  • The unit of energy. Its symbol is J.

  • Energy cannot be created or destroyed. It can only be transferred.

Energy is transferred between stores in four ways.

  • By a force: a push or pull moves something.
  • By an electric current flowing in a circuit.
  • By heating: from a hotter object to a cooler one.
  • By waves: light and sound carry energy away.

Light, sound and electric current are pathways. They are not stores.

Light from a lamp hits the walls. It warms them very slightly.

Energy is measured in joules (J).

1 kilojoule (kJ) is 1000 J.

  • A catapult fires: its elastic potential store empties. The stone's kinetic store fills.
  • A diver falls: the gravitational potential store empties. The kinetic store fills.
  • A car brakes: the kinetic store empties. The brakes' thermal store fills.
  • You run after breakfast: the food's chemical store empties. Your kinetic and thermal stores fill.

Energy cannot be created or destroyed.

It can only be transferred from one store to another.

Count every store, including the surroundings. The total is the same before and after.

This is the law of conservation of energy.

The rolling ball had energy in its kinetic store.

Friction with the carpet slowed it down.

This warmed the ball and the carpet very slightly.

So the energy went into thermal stores. None of it was used up.

  • Wrong: Energy gets used up.

    Right: Energy is never used up. It is transferred to other stores.

  • Wrong: A battery stores electrical energy.

    Right: A battery has a chemical store. The electric current is a pathway.

  • Wrong: Light and sound are energy stores.

    Right: They are pathways. They carry energy from one store to another.

Key fact

Energy is held in stores, such as kinetic, thermal and chemical. It is transferred between stores by forces, electric currents, heating and waves. Energy cannot be created or destroyed, so the total stays the same.

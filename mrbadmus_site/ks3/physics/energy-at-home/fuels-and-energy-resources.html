Key words

  • A fuel made from the remains of ancient living things. Coal, oil and natural gas.

  • An energy resource that will run out. It is not replaced as fast as we use it.

  • An energy resource that is replaced naturally, so it will not run out.

  • Something we use to supply energy, such as a fuel, wind or sunlight.

Key knowledge

The three fossil fuels are coal, oil and natural gas.

They are chemical stores of energy.

Burning them releases energy, and carbon dioxide.

They took millions of years to form, so they are non-renewable.

Coal formed from plants in swamp forests, millions of years ago.

Oil and natural gas formed from tiny sea organisms, such as plankton.

When they died, they were buried under layers of mud and sand.

There was little oxygen, so they did not rot away.

Heat and pressure over millions of years turned them into fossil fuels.

Fossil fuels will run out.

Burning them releases carbon dioxide, a greenhouse gas.

This adds to global warming.

Burning coal and oil can also release sulfur dioxide, which causes acid rain.

Renewable resources are the main alternative to fossil fuels.

Renewable only means the resource will not run out.

It does not mean it gives off no carbon dioxide.

Wood and biofuels are renewable, but burning them releases carbon dioxide.

New plants take in some of this carbon dioxide as they grow.

Nuclear power is non-renewable. It needs uranium, which will run out.

But it releases almost no carbon dioxide while it makes electricity.

Nuclear power makes radioactive waste, which must be stored safely for a very long time.

Wind and solar depend on the weather and the time of day.

Demand for electricity does not fall when the wind drops.

So something else must cover the gap:

  • storing energy, for example in large batteries
  • power stations that run whatever the weather, such as nuclear or gas
  • building extra wind and solar farms in different places

Each of these costs money.

  • Wrong: Renewable means clean.

    Right: Renewable means it will not run out. Burning wood still releases carbon dioxide.

  • Wrong: Nuclear power releases lots of carbon dioxide.

    Right: It releases almost none while it runs. Its problem is radioactive waste.

  • Wrong: Electricity is an energy resource.

    Right: Electricity is made using energy resources, such as gas or wind.

Key fact

Coal, oil and natural gas are fossil fuels. They formed over millions of years, so they are non-renewable. Renewable resources, such as wind, solar and hydroelectric, will not run out. Renewable does not always mean no carbon dioxide.

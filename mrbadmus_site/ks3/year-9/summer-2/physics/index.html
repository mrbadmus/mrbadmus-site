Space

Gravity never switches off and never pushes. Follow it from a bathroom scale that measures newtons and prints kilograms, out to the pull between the Earth and the Sun, and on to a ruler made of light.

  2. 05Seasons and the tiltModelThe Earth is five million kilometres closer to the Sun in January than in July, and January is the cold one. Whatever causes seasons, it is not how far away we are.Open this lesson
  3. 06How far is a light year?QuantitativeIt sounds like a length of time and it is a distance. Getting that one word straight is the difference between understanding the scale of the universe and having no idea at all.Open this lesson

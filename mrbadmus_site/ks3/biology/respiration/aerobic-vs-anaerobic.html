Respiration · Contrast

Aerobic vs anaerobic

Start here

Cells can release energy from glucose with oxygen, or without it.

If you had to guess, which way releases energy faster?

The one without oxygen. It releases energy faster, but much less of it.

The one without oxygen. It releases energy faster, but much less of it.

Key words

  • Respiration that uses oxygen. It releases energy from glucose.

  • Respiration without oxygen. It releases much less energy from each glucose.

Both are respiration.

Both break down glucose.

Both release energy that the cell can use.

Both are chemical reactions controlled by enzymes.

Most of the time, your cells respire aerobically.

In a sprint, anaerobic respiration releases extra energy quickly. Lactic acid builds up.

For ten seconds, that is fine.

A marathon runner paces the race, so the oxygen supply keeps up.

They stay mostly aerobic, so lactic acid does not build up.

Aerobic respiration gets much more energy from each glucose, which suits a long race.

Some bacteria are killed by oxygen. They live in places with none, such as mud.

They respire only anaerobically, so they get little energy from each glucose.

So they need more food for the same work, or they are less active.

Early Earth had almost no oxygen in the air.

So anaerobic respiration is probably the older process.

The oxygen in the air was added later, by photosynthesis.

  • Wrong: Anaerobic respiration releases more energy.

    Right: It releases energy faster, but much less from each glucose.

  • Wrong: In humans, both kinds make carbon dioxide.

    Right: Anaerobic respiration in humans makes lactic acid only.

  • Wrong: Anaerobic respiration only happens when something goes wrong.

    Right: Yeast uses it whenever oxygen is short. Some bacteria use it all the time.

Key fact

Aerobic respiration uses oxygen and breaks glucose down completely. It releases much more energy from each glucose. Anaerobic respiration needs no oxygen. It releases much less energy, but it releases it faster. Aerobic makes carbon dioxide and water. Anaerobic makes lactic acid, or ethanol and carbon dioxide.

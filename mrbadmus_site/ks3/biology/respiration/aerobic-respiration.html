Respiration · Process

Aerobic respiration

Start here

A person loses 10 kg of fat over a year.

If you had to guess, how does most of that fat leave the body?

Out through the lungs. Most of it leaves as carbon dioxide in your breath.

Key words

  • Respiration that uses oxygen. It releases energy from glucose.

  • Tiny parts of a cell where aerobic respiration happens.

  • A sugar. It is the fuel cells use in respiration.

  • Moving air in and out of the lungs. It is not respiration.

Key knowledge

Glucose and oxygen are the reactants.

Carbon dioxide and water are the products.

The reaction releases energy. Energy is not a substance, so it is not a product.

Every living cell needs energy, all the time.

Respiration releases the energy stored in glucose.

Cells use this energy for:

  • moving, such as muscles contracting
  • keeping warm
  • building larger molecules for growth and repair
  • taking in substances by active transport

Respiration never stops. A cell that stops respiring soon dies.

Aerobic respiration happens in the mitochondria.

Almost every cell has mitochondria, in plants and in animals.

Cells that need a lot of energy, like muscle cells, have many.

Working muscles need more energy, so they respire faster.

They use more glucose and oxygen, and make more carbon dioxide.

You breathe faster and deeper to take in oxygen and remove carbon dioxide.

Your heart beats faster to carry blood to the muscles faster.

Atoms are not made or destroyed in a reaction.

25 g of glucose reacts with 26.7 g of oxygen.

This makes 36.7 g of carbon dioxide and 15.0 g of water.

So 51.7 g goes in and 51.7 g comes out.

When you lose fat, most of its mass is breathed out as carbon dioxide.

Germinating seeds respire fast, to release energy for growth.

In a sealed flask, they warm up, because respiration releases some energy as heat.

They use up oxygen and give out carbon dioxide.

Boiled seeds are dead. Nothing changes in their flask.

  • Wrong: Respiration means breathing.

    Right: Respiration is a reaction in cells. Breathing brings in the oxygen it uses.

  • Wrong: Respiration makes energy.

    Right: Energy cannot be made. Respiration releases energy stored in glucose.

  • Wrong: Fat turns into energy and disappears.

    Right: Its atoms leave the body, mostly as carbon dioxide you breathe out.

  • Wrong: Plants do not respire.

    Right: Plants respire all the time, just like animals.

Key fact

Aerobic respiration is a reaction in the mitochondria of cells. Glucose and oxygen react to make carbon dioxide and water. This releases the energy cells need to stay alive.

Respiration releases it in many small steps, at body temperature.

Enzymes control each step.

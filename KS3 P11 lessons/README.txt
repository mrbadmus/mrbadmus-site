Alongside them:
  index.dc.html   unit contents page — the offline front door for this folder
  Cfifa.dc.html   the shared five-step worked-example block
  Bench.dc.html   the shared bench shell (tabs, slider, bars, readouts)
  support.js      the runtime every page loads
  _ds/            the MrBadmusAI design system (tokens, fonts, component CSS)

P11 is a referencing unit. It owns these four lessons and pulls the rest of its
statutory coverage from Chemistry C1 and C4; the Connects-to blocks link out to
those units rather than repeating them.

p11-01 is the unit's only QUANTITATIVE lesson and carries the full CFIFA block:
Convert, Formula, Insert, Fine-tune, Answer, revealed one step at a time, with
two worked examples and two student attempts. The other three are MODEL and
CONTRAST lessons with no calculation in them, so no worked example has been
invented to fill the slot.

Every lesson carries four rail stops, a four-rung mastery ladder, two
misconception quotes, one [data-key-fact] block and a key note.

Delivery notes are in NOTES-P11-P12.md.

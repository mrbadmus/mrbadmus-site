Triple

Decomposers are living organisms, so how fast decay happens depends on their conditions. Temperature: warmer is faster, up to an optimum. Water: they need it to survive and grow. Oxygen: most decomposers respire aerobically; without oxygen, decay is slower, and anaerobic decay produces methane. Gardeners make compost, a natural fertiliser, by giving decomposers the best conditions. A biogas generator keeps waste anaerobic on purpose and collects the methane as a fuel.

Required practical · RP10 Biology

Milk decays faster in the warm. Time it.

  1. In a test tube, mix 5 cm³ of full-fat milk, 7 cm³ of sodium carbonate solution and 5 drops of phenolphthalein. The alkaline mixture turns pink.
  2. Put 1 cm³ of lipase solution in a second tube. Stand both tubes in a water bath at the test temperature for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the lipase to the milk, stir, and start the stopwatch.
  4. Stop the clock when the pink colour has gone. Record the time.
  5. Repeat at other temperatures, such as 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60 °C, and repeat each to calculate a mean.

Variables. Independent: the temperature. Dependent: the time for the pink to go. Control: the volumes and concentrations of milk, lipase and sodium carbonate, and the number of drops of indicator.

Risks. Phenolphthalein is dissolved in ethanol, which is flammable and irritant: no flames, and wear eye protection. Sodium carbonate solution irritates eyes. Lipase can cause allergic reactions, so avoid skin contact. Never taste the milk.

Equations · rates of decay

Learn it

rate of decay = mass lost ÷ time

mass lost = starting mass − final mass. In g per week, the time must be in weeks.

Learn it

rate = 1 ÷ time

For the milk practical: time in seconds, rate in s⁻¹.

Key fact

Microorganisms break down dead material. They respire, returning carbon to the atmosphere as carbon dioxide, and they return mineral ions to the soil.

Key note · AQA 4.7.2.2–4.7.2.3 (8461) · RP10

Decomposition

  1. 01Microorganisms respire, returning carbon to the atmosphere as carbon dioxide.
  2. 02Breaking down dead material returns mineral ions, such as nitrate, to the soil for plant roots to absorb.
  3. 03Decay is faster when it is warm (up to an optimum), moist, and there is oxygen.
  4. 04Anaerobic decay produces methane; biogas generators collect it as a fuel. Compost is a natural fertiliser.
  5. 05RP10: milk + sodium carbonate + phenolphthalein, then lipase; time until the pink goes. Rate = 1 ÷ time.

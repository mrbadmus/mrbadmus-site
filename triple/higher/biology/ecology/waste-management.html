Waste management and pollution

More people, each using more, means more waste. Badly handled, it pollutes water, air and land, and every one of the three kills living things.

Two drivers

More people, and more per person.

The human population is growing rapidly, and the standard of living is rising. Both mean more resources are used and more waste is produced. Unless waste and chemical materials are properly handled, more pollution will be caused. Change each driver and watch the waste.

Sources

Sewage; fertiliser washed off fields; toxic chemicals.

Effect on living things

Kills organisms living in rivers, lakes and seas.

Biodiversity

The variety of all the different species of organisms in an ecosystem. Pollution kills plants and animals, which can reduce biodiversity: it falls when species are lost from the area.

Handled properly

Treating sewage, filtering gases from chimneys and recycling all cut pollution. How sewage is treated is in Potable water.
